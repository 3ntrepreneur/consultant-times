Canadian holiday forecasts split: Deloitte sees gift spending down 5%, Mastercard sees sales up 3.5%

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Deloitte Canada's 2026 Holiday Retail Outlook, released 14 October, projects spending on gifts and gift cards to fall 5% and says Canadians are becoming 'far more discerning'. One in three (34%) say their household finances are worse than a year ago. It calls Black Friday the defining conversion moment of the season.

Mastercard's Economics Institute expects Canadian holiday retail sales, excluding gas and autos, to rise 3.5% year over year between 1 November and Boxing Day. The measures differ: Deloitte tracks planned gift spending from a survey, while Mastercard tracks sales activity.

Retail Insider quotes an industry executive saying Black Friday has become a shopping season rather than a single day, with some categories starting promotions in early November. Black Friday falls on 27 November.

What it means: Retailers must plan promotions, inventory and pricing around a cautious shopper. A wrong forecast means either empty shelves or excess stock.

What we don't know yet

  • Forecasts differ in method and may not reconcile.
