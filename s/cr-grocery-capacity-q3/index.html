Canadian grocers keep adding stores and capital spending while real supermarket volumes stay soft

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Retail Insider's Q3 grocery report says Canada's largest grocers kept adding and repositioning capacity even as supermarket data showed limited volume growth. Loblaw announced $2.4 billion of planned Canadian capital spending for 2026 and said in September that about $1.2 billion remained to be deployed. Discount expansion is being adapted to local markets.

Loblaw also completed the sale of PC Financial on 1 July for total consideration of $1,234 million, including 7.2 million EQB shares valued at $963 million, leaving it with about 19.9% of EQB. Loblaw's third-quarter results are due on 19 November.

What it means: Overbuilding capacity into flat volume squeezes the weakest chains. Format, location and price-position decisions decide share.
