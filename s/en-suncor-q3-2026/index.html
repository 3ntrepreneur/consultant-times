Suncor's profit more than doubles and it lifts its buyback by half

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Suncor reported third-quarter 2026 net earnings of $3.814 billion, or $3.27 a share, up from $1.619 billion a year earlier. Adjusted operating earnings were $4.631 billion and adjusted funds from operations $6.705 billion. Net earnings include a $1.080 billion non-cash asset impairment.

Oil sands production was 948,000 barrels a day against 958,300 a year earlier, as extra maintenance at Syncrude offset a record third quarter at Firebag. Refined product sales hit a third-quarter record of 676,900 barrels a day, helped by wider export capacity. OilPrice reports Suncor raised planned monthly buybacks by 50% to $750 million from October and expects $5.5 billion of repurchases in 2026.

What it means: Integrated oil sands and refining shows how much margin sits downstream. Capital-return policy and maintenance execution are the discussion points for boards.
