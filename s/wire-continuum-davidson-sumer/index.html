Boutique adviser Continuum hires ex-Jefferies banker Graham Davidson, taking its UK team to 50; it is advising on TPG's recapitalisation of Sumer (Consultancy.uk).

Reported

Open in the full site →

Continuum Advisory Partners has appointed Graham Davidson as a senior managing director in its UK team, according to Consultancy.uk on 26 November 2026. The report says he has more than 25 years in debt, equity and M&A, and was previously a managing director at Jefferies and a partner at Perella Weinberg Partners.

The article says Continuum has completed 18 transactions in 2026 and is advising Sumer and Penta Capital on the recapitalisation of Sumer by TPG, which it describes as valuing the UK mid-market accountancy at 1 billion pounds enterprise value. The hire brings the team to 50 professionals, ahead of a stated target of 50 by January 2027.

The Sumer figure comes from the trade report only; no deal release was read, so terms and timing are unconfirmed.
