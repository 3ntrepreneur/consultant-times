Stablecoin payments reach about $482 billion in nine months as card networks and banks plug in

Developing · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Figures reported this month attribute the $482 billion payments total and 45% growth to Allium, a blockchain analytics firm. The same coverage says Mastercard launched a platform that combines fiat and stablecoin balances, and Stripe partnered with the Plasma network on cards that span currencies.

Trackers put the overall stablecoin market at roughly $280 billion at the start of September, though the number depends on methodology. Separately, Coinbase received conditional approval in April for a national trust bank charter from the US Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, a sign regulators are bringing stablecoin infrastructure inside the banking perimeter.

Treat the payments total with care: analytics firms define 'payments' differently and some volume is trading activity. The direction of travel, incumbents wrapping stablecoins rather than fighting them, is better supported than the exact number.

What it means: Payments strategy is moving from 'will stablecoins matter' to 'where do we sit in the chain'. Banks, processors and large merchants will want help choosing between building, partnering and staying out.

What we don't know yet

  • The payments figure comes from one analytics firm reported by an aggregator; we could not check the method.
  • Share of volume that is consumer or business payments.
