CrowdStrike agrees to buy Zscaler for $43.9 billion in stock

Confirmed · 3 min

Open in the full site →

CrowdStrike announced on 23 November 2026 that it will buy Zscaler at 1.135 CrowdStrike shares per Zscaler share, about $260.41 each on 20 November prices. Zscaler holders would own about 15.9% of the combined company. Closing needs a Zscaler shareholder vote and regulatory approvals.

CrowdStrike targets about $1 billion of annualised revenue run-rate within 30 months and $500 million of annualised cost savings within 18 to 24 months, according to its release; both are company targets. See the full story in the front-page edition.

What it means: Integration planning, $500M cost take-out delivery, vendor consolidation and CISO platform-strategy work on both sides of the deal.

  • Regulatory review scope
