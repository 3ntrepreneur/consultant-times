KPMG hires two Deloitte tax partners and team in East Africa

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

KPMG has strengthened its East Africa tax advisory practice with hires from Deloitte, according to Consultancy.africa on 20 November 2026. On 11 November it announced partners Fred Omondi and Fredrick Kimotho and associate director Kennedy Okoyo; it later confirmed Patrick Chege as associate director.

The article cites The Kenya Times as reporting that at least six more professionals moved from Deloitte to KPMG. All hires are based in Kenya. KPMG East Africa has more than 1,000 professionals and 45 partners across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda, per the report.

Omondi has 23 years of experience and previously worked at the Kenya Revenue Authority; Kimotho has 12-plus years in tax advisory and policy; Okoyo has 12-plus years in indirect tax; Chege spent about 25 years at the Kenya Revenue Authority. KPMG said the appointments strengthen its ability to support complex client engagements.

Deloitte's response and the number of clients moving were not reported.

What it means: Tax talent moves signal where indirect-tax and policy advisory demand sits in Kenya; clients should expect continuity checks as teams change firms.
