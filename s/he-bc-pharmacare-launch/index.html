B.C. launches national pharmacare coverage for diabetes drugs and menopause therapy

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

The B.C. government said its PharmaCare National Pharmacare Plan launched for residents on 1 March 2026 and covers the full cost of many diabetes medications and menopausal hormone therapy. Lifetimes Canada says Manitoba, P.E.I. and Yukon have also signed agreements and are phasing in their own timelines.

The same explainer notes new Patented Medicine Prices Review Board guidelines took effect on 1 January 2026 and that the CUSMA renegotiation could affect drug spending if intellectual-property protections for brands lengthen.

What it means: Pharmacies, insurers and drug makers face a larger public payer for chronic-disease drugs. Private plan design and pricing follow.
