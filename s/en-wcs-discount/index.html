Heavy-oil discount blows out to $31, then narrows as freight eases

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Reuters, via the BOE Report, says the WCS discount at Hardisty reached as wide as $31 a barrel below WTI in the first week of November and narrowed to $25 on 9 November. At Houston, WCS hit an all-time wide discount of $23 in late October as high global freight rates cut demand for re-exports.

Analyst Rory Johnston attributes the retreat mainly to freight rates easing from October highs. Cenovus said on 4 November that exporting off the B.C. coast is currently more attractive than sending barrels to the US Gulf Coast, and expects the wide spread to be temporary. East Daley Analytics notes planned Trans Mountain and Enbridge expansions are not expected before 2027.

What it means: A wide discount hits producer cash flow and royalties. It also raises the value of any spare west-coast export capacity.
