Lilly's sales jump 39% on obesity drugs as prices fall 15%

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Eli Lilly reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $24.4 billion, up 39%, driven by a 53% increase in volume partly offset by a 15% decline in realized prices. Mounjaro and Zepbound together brought in more than $16 billion. Non-GAAP earnings per share were $7.48, including $4.57 of acquired research charges tied to deals such as AtaiBeckley and Curevo.

Lilly raised 2026 revenue guidance to $91 billion to $92 billion and cut non-GAAP EPS guidance slightly to $35 to $36 because of those charges. The call also flagged about $900 million of one-time revenue items, including milestones and rebate estimate changes. Formal 2027 guidance is due in January.

What it means: Volume is outrunning price in obesity care. Payers, pharmacies and rivals need models for a lower-price, higher-volume market.

What we don't know yet

  • 2027 guidance is not out until January.
  • Zacks' reading of the guidance revision is secondary.
