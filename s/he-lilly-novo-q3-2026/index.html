Lilly's obesity lead widens in Q3 as Novo leans on its Wegovy pill

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Life Science Daily's roundup of third-quarter pharma results has Lilly at $24.4bn of revenue, against $17.6bn a year earlier, and above its earlier guidance range of $85bn to $87bn. Lilly's presentation says more than 500,000 patients now get its obesity medicines through the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge, and about 80% of them are new to this drug class.

On IQVIA prescription data for the four weeks to 25 September, Lilly held 63.0% of US incretin prescriptions and Novo Nordisk 36.6%. Novo's adjusted sales rose 4% at constant exchange rates to DKK 79.2bn, while adjusted operating profit rose 27%. Its Wegovy pill brought in DKK 5.4bn, DKK 4.2bn of it in the US, and Novo estimates it takes roughly 80% of new-to-brand pill starts.

Reuters reported that Novo raised its 2026 outlook for the third time this year. The two companies are growing for different reasons: Lilly on volume and share, Novo on a new format and a recovering position.

What it means: Obesity is now a two-player market with a clear leader. Payers, pharmacy chains and digital-health firms are modelling how price cuts and Medicare coverage shift volume between the two.

What we don't know yet

  • Net pricing after rebates and cash-pay discounts is not disclosed by product.
  • Share figures are one four-week prescription window, not a full-quarter view.
