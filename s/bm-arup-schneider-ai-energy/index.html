Arup and Schneider Electric partner on AI software for energy infrastructure

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Engineering and design consultancy Arup and Schneider Electric have agreed a partnership to develop AI-enabled software and physical systems, according to Consultancy.uk on 26 November 2026. The target areas are energy infrastructure, urban transformation, sustainable development and healthcare.

The report says one use is predicting cooling-system failures and maintenance needs for data-centre operators, and that Schneider is adding generative AI to ETAP, its electrical power design and operations platform. The two firms earlier worked together on predictive maintenance for Schneider's Asset Advisor.

Fiona Cousins of Arup and Frederic Godemel of Schneider are quoted in the report. No financial terms, timeline or customers were disclosed.

What it means: Data-centre cooling and power-resilience programmes, asset-management analytics and digital-twin design; engineering consultancies moving into software-led services.
