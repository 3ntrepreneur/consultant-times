Oliver Wyman to buy 1,200-person Synpulse in its largest deal

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Oliver Wyman, the management consultancy in the Marsh McLennan group, has agreed to acquire Synpulse, a financial services consultancy with about 1,200 professionals across Europe, Asia and North America, according to Consultancy.eu on 19 November 2026. The publication describes it as the largest acquisition in Oliver Wyman's history. No price or deal terms were disclosed, and the transaction is expected to close in early 2027.

Synpulse was founded in 1996 and sells consulting and technology services: enterprise systems modernisation, digital product development and AI solution implementation. It also runs a technology division, Synpulse8, which works with more than 100 technology partners. The firm is co-headquartered in Switzerland and Singapore, with about 550 staff in each, and has roughly 100 people in the United States and Canada, per Consultancy.eu.

The deal is Oliver Wyman's second acquisition this year, after the US restructuring firm CR3 Partners. Consultancy.eu puts Oliver Wyman at more than 7,000 staff in over 70 offices, which would pass 8,000 people once Synpulse is included. Synpulse is to keep its brand and leadership team, and the article gives no long-term integration plan. Ted Moynihan, Oliver Wyman's chief executive, and Konrad Niggli and Yves Roesti, Synpulse's co-chief executives, are quoted in the announcement coverage.

For readers in consulting, the logic is visible in the target's profile. Strategy firms have long sold the plan and left the build to systems integrators; a 1,200-person firm that modernises banking systems and implements AI gives Oliver Wyman a delivery arm inside its core sector. For clients, it means one provider can now be asked to carry a programme from target-state design to implementation, and procurement teams will want to know how independence is handled where the same group advises and builds.

What is not yet known is the price, how Synpulse's Asian and Swiss client base overlaps with Oliver Wyman's, and whether other partnerships held by Synpulse8 survive intact. We have read the Consultancy.eu report only, not a company release.

What it means: Integration, brand and talent-retention work for the combined firm; for banks and insurers, a new option for strategy-to-build programmes and a vendor-landscape check on who now owns delivery.

What we don't know yet

  • Any regulatory approvals needed before the early-2027 close
  • How the Synpulse8 technology partnerships will be handled after closing
