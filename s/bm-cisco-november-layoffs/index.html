Cisco makes fresh layoffs, cutting under 1% of workforce

Developing · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Cisco's latest round of cuts affects less than 1% of its global workforce, according to People Matters on 20 November 2026. The report, based on LinkedIn posts from affected employees, says the cuts happened on 18 November and hit US staff. Cisco did not disclose the teams, locations or number affected, and the article cites a Cisco statement to SDxCentral that winning in the AI era requires focused discipline.

The article puts the longer trend in context: headcount fell by about 8,000, or 8.8%, between July 2024 and July 2026, and Cisco cut about 4,000 jobs in May 2026. It says Cisco expects revenue per employee to rise about 19%, with chief executive Chuck Robbins citing $19 billion in incremental revenue.

People Matters says there is no evidence linking the November cuts to Cisco's work with OpenAI Codex. Because the account rests on employee posts and a statement reported second-hand, the confidence here is Developing.
