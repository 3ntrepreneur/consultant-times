Medicare's GLP-1 Bridge puts a $50 copay on weight-loss drugs until the end of 2027

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

CMS says the Bridge covers Wegovy (injection and tablet), the Zepbound KwikPen and Foundayo for weight loss or weight maintenance. The copay is a flat $50 for a 30-day supply regardless of dose, applies nationwide, and sits outside the normal Part D benefit for the length of the demonstration.

There are limits. People already getting a GLP-1 under Part D for conditions such as type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea are not eligible, the copay does not count toward the deductible or out-of-pocket cap, and Extra Help amounts do not apply. Early reports described pharmacy denials in the first days.

A separate CMS model, announced in December 2025, would let Part D plans and state Medicaid programs cover GLP-1s at negotiated prices, with Part D participation from January 2027 and voluntary for manufacturers, states and plans.

What it means: Payers, pharmacies and manufacturers are modelling uptake, prior-authorization workload and plan bids. The sunset date creates a clear planning cliff.
