Teneo hires Roland Berger's nuclear lead Mike Granowski for energy practice

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Teneo has hired Mike Granowski as a senior managing director in its energy and infrastructure practice, according to Consulting.us on 20 November 2026. He will be based in Chicago and support clients in nuclear energy and the broader power and gas sector.

Granowski has more than 25 years of consulting experience. He comes from Roland Berger, where he spent seven years as a partner, global nuclear technology lead and North American energy and utilities platform lead. Earlier roles included Enovation Partners, Bridge Strategy Group and Navigant, and he began as a nuclear engineer at Commonwealth Edison.

Dan Gabaldon, Teneo's vice chairman for management consulting and head of energy, welcomed the hire. Consulting.us puts Teneo at more than 1,800 people in over 50 offices.

The article does not say what client work the hire is meant to win or whether others from Roland Berger are following.

What it means: Shows demand for nuclear and power-sector advice and the talent race for it; utilities and developers get another adviser with nuclear depth.
