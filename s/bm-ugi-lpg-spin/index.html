UGI plans to spin off its global LPG business, separating from its regulated utilities

Confirmed · 2 min

Open in the full site →

According to UGI's release of 18 November, the new LPG company would be the largest publicly listed LPG distributor in the world and the largest US retail propane distributor, while New UGI keeps regulated gas utilities in Pennsylvania and West Virginia plus midstream assets. Shareholders would receive LPG shares pro rata in a transaction intended to be tax-free, and New UGI may retain up to 19.9% initially. J.P. Morgan and Latham & Watkins are advising.

The Philadelphia Inquirer reports UGI shares fell 8% on 19 November to $35.18, nearly erasing the gain from a KKR approach in August at $42.50 a share, and that CEO Bob Flexon said he is not completely opposed to selling the whole company for a better offer.

The release says final board approval, an effective Form 10 and debt consents are required and that there is no assurance the separation will occur.

What it means: Separation planning, Form 10 and capital-structure design, TSA and stand-alone cost work, and valuation advice as an energy group splits propane from utilities.

What we don't know yet

  • Leadership, capital structure and dividend policy for each company are to be detailed later
  • Whether a whole-company bid re-emerges is unclear
