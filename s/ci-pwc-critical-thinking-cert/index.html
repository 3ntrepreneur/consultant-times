PwC US will certify its 85,000 people in critical thinking for the AI era

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Bloomberg Law reports that PwC US will launch a certification in critical thinking for its more than 85,000 partners and staff, intended to keep human judgment central as AI tools take on more client work.

The programme uses personalised simulations, for example reviewing an AI-generated internal-controls assessment and challenging its conclusions, and opens with a diagnostic of each person's thinking style. Rollout starts in December across US and Mexico offices and offshore centres such as India and is due to finish by June 2027. PwC says it plans to offer the framework to universities from early 2027.
