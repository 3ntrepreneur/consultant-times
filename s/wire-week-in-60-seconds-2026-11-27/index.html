The week in five stories: CrowdStrike's $43.9 billion Zscaler deal, America's Car-Mart's Chapter 11, Nvidia's CEO reversal, Clariant and CD&R's Ashland talks, and EY Canada's $80 million innovation bet.

CrowdStrike agreed to buy Zscaler in an all-stock deal valued at about $43.9 billion, with targets of $500 million in cost savings and $1 billion in revenue synergies, the week's largest transaction and a template for security-platform consolidation. America's Car-Mart filed for Chapter 11 with a $10 million stalking-horse bid, a reminder that subprime auto lending stress creates restructuring work.

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive 11 days after he resigned, putting governance and succession planning in front of every AI-exposed client. Clariant and CD&R confirmed talks to buy Ashland, a possible large chemicals deal that would bring diligence and integration work. EY Canada committed $80 million to AI, quantum and future-of-work bets, a signal of how the large firms are investing in their own capability.
