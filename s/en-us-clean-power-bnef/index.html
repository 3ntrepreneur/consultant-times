Data centres push US clean-power additions to records in 2026 and 2027

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

BloombergNEF forecasts the US will add 89 GW of solar, wind and storage capacity in 2026 and 93 GW in 2027, two straight record years. It expects more than 1 terawatt of additions from 2026 to 2036. The storage outlook rose 34% to 383 GW and onshore wind rose 15% to about 100 GW, while utility-scale solar fell 7.5% to 430 GW because of new import tariffs.

BNEF says surging data-centre demand is also opening room for new gas generation. A pipeline of tax-credit-eligible projects underpins near-term build, but protectionism and credit timing are risks.

What it means: Utilities, developers and power-intensive clients plan around a record build driven by data-centre demand. Canadian suppliers and investors read this as export-market signal.

What we don't know yet

  • Forecasts, not completed builds.
