AI is pushing the big firms toward outcome-based fees and expert-heavy teams

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

McKinsey's UK managing partner, Michael Birshan, told reporters in London that the firm is doing more performance-based work, per Business Insider. That fee mix figure is from late 2025, so check for updates.

The shift matters for the pyramid. If tools handle first-pass analysis, firms need fewer junior analysts per project and more people with deep domain skills. Bain also moved ahead of BCG into second place in Vault's 2026 North American prestige ranking, a rare change at the top.

What it means: If AI compresses analytical hours, pricing tied to hours loses its logic. Firms and clients are renegotiating scope, risk-sharing and value metrics.
