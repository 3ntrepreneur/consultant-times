Canadian export pipelines run tight: Mainline at 16% apportionment, Trans Mountain at a record 7%

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

S&P Global reports that Enbridge's Mainline set heavy-crude apportionment at 16% at Superior and 7% at Kerrobert for November, with light crude at 19% and 13% at two delivery points. Heavy apportionment has been high all year, rising from 13% in January to 19% in June. Mainline throughput averaged 3.1 million barrels a day in the second quarter.

Trans Mountain's 890,000-barrel-a-day system was apportioned 7% for October, a system record and the third time this year. Growing Asian demand is pulling more barrels off the B.C. coast.

What it means: Producers, marketers and refiners should treat takeaway capacity as the binding constraint. It shapes netbacks, expansion timing and Asia strategy.

What we don't know yet

  • Apportionment moves monthly and may differ by delivery point.
