Paramount closes its Warner Bros. Discovery takeover and the combined company becomes Skydance

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Paramount completed its acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery on 6 October. Law Commentary says it closed after the deal cleared federal antitrust review and Paramount settled suits brought by a coalition of states and the Writers Guild. WBD shareholders received about $31 a share in cash. The combined company is called Skydance.

The headline value depends on the measure: AP reports an $81 billion takeover, while other outlets cite an enterprise value of about $110 billion. Advanced Television says the company promises at least 30 theatrical films a year with a minimum 45-day window. Financing from Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the UAE drew scrutiny.

What it means: The sector is now run by fewer, larger studios. Integration, cost take-out, streaming scale and regulatory-compliance work follow.
