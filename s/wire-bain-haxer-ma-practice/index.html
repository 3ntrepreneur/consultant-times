Bain names Jeff Haxer global head of its M&A and Divestitures practice, succeeding Les Baird.

Open in the full site →

Bain & Company says it has appointed Jeff Haxer, based in Chicago, as global head of its M&A and Divestitures practice. He succeeds Les Baird, who led the practice from 2018.

Sinead Mullen, based in Denver, becomes the Americas regional practice lead, succeeding Colleen von Eckartsberg. Bain says the practice more than doubled in size under Baird and built Signal by Bain, an AI-powered programme-management platform for integrations and separations.

For clients, leadership continuity in a deal practice matters as M&A activity picks up; the release does not give financial details.
