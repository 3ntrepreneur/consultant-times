Thomson Reuters launches a US$500M share buyback and a new normal course issuer bid for up to 7 million shares.

Confirmed

Open in the full site →

Thomson Reuters said on 11 November 2026 that it will repurchase up to US$500 million of its shares and has set up a new normal course issuer bid (NCIB), the Canadian mechanism that lets a listed company buy back stock on the open market within set limits.

According to the company's release, the bid covers up to 7 million common shares, about 1.6% of the 433,458,865 shares outstanding at 31 October 2026. It runs from 13 November 2026 to 12 November 2027, and purchases can be made on the TSX, the Nasdaq Global Select Market and other eligible venues. The TSX daily limit is 181,938 shares.

Woodbridge, which holds about 70% of the company, will take part proportionally, with purchases from it starting on 13 November under a TSX exemption. The release says the company bought 6,155,886 shares for US$600 million, an average of US$97.48, under its previous bid. It describes the programme as a flexible way to return capital and says keeping a healthy float is in all shareholders' interest.
