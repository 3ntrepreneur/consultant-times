Merck KGaA agrees to buy Rapid Micro Biosystems at $0.75 a share

Confirmed · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Rapid Micro Biosystems said on 16 November 2026 that it signed a definitive agreement to be acquired by Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany. Under the agreement, a Merck KGaA subsidiary, EMD Holdings NewCo II, Inc., will launch a tender offer for all shares at $0.75 in cash, followed by a merger.

The company's release says closing is expected in December 2026, subject to a minimum tender condition and customary conditions; it does not specify regulatory approvals. Perella Weinberg Partners is the company's financial adviser and Goodwin Procter LLP its legal counsel. InsideArbitrage, an event-driven deal newsletter, puts the premium at 15.38% and the deal value at about $39 million; the release itself does not state either figure, so we treat them as the newsletter's calculation.

The deal is small by value but it is a clear pattern: a strategic buyer in life-science tools picking up a quality-control automation business at a low absolute price. For consultants, the work is in integration of manufacturing-quality products into a larger life-science portfolio, customer-overlap analysis and the buyer's own technology roadmap. What the release does not say is why Merck KGaA wants the business or what it plans for the product line, so those are unknowns.

What it means: Deal-readiness, integration and carve-in work for life-science tools buyers; distressed small-cap targets are being absorbed by strategics.

What we don't know yet

  • Merck KGaA's strategic rationale and integration plans
  • Regulatory approvals required
