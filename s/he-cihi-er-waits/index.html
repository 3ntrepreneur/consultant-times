CIHI says emergency waits are driven by long-term care and home-care backlogs, not the ER itself

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

The Canadian Institute for Health Information said on 24 June that long emergency department waits are made worse by a lack of available spots in long-term care and home care, creating a bottleneck for hospital beds. The institute's director of health system analytics said improving ER waits will take solutions that look at the entire health-care system.

A commentary in Canadian Healthcare Technology agrees ED crowding is a system issue but argues the report focuses only on throughput, a process measure that does not explain why the system is failing or whether patient outcomes are improving.

What it means: Capacity planning, patient-flow and long-term-care investment are the levers. Hospital and provincial clients need whole-system, not ER-only, fixes.

What we don't know yet

  • The sources do not give the national wait-time figures.
