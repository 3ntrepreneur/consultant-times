BDO hires EY and PwC private-client tax specialists as directors for its London US-UK practice, citing a 31% rise in cross-border M&A.

Reported

Open in the full site →

BDO has hired Thomas Gaughan, from EY, and Daniel Martinez, from PwC, as directors in its London-based US-UK private client tax practice, according to Consultancy.uk on 20 November 2026. Gaughan focuses on pre-arrival and departure planning, residence and asset structuring and succession; Martinez on US and UK taxation of private equity and investment professionals, including carried interest and pensions.

The article cites Oliver Wyman research that cross-border M&A is up 31% year to date, with more than half of it, worth $461 billion in the first half, running through the transatlantic corridor. Those figures are Oliver Wyman's, as reported, not BDO's. Start dates were not stated.
