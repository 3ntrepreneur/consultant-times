The STB restarts its review of the $85 billion Union Pacific-Norfolk Southern merger

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

FreightWaves reports the Surface Transportation Board removed the proceeding from abeyance and adopted a schedule that pushes a final decision well into the second half of 2027. Final briefs are due 28 May 2027. The review is the first under the 2001 rules, which require applicants to show the merger enhances competition.

Pulse 2.0 reports comments, protests and requests for conditions on the revised application are due 18 November 2026, and the Board expects a public hearing before final briefs. The decision is procedural, not a ruling on the merits.

What it means: Shippers, ports and rival railroads must decide their position now. It is also a test of the tougher 2001 rail-merger rules.

What we don't know yet

  • Hearing dates are still to be set.
