Canada-US trade talks collapse and 50% US tariffs hit $20 billion of Canadian goods

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

The CNN report carried by KRDO says talks failed on 21 August, the night before the deadline for 50% US tariffs on $20 billion of Canadian goods. The National reports the tariffs cover hundreds of goods, including plywood, liquor, electrical equipment and hockey gear, and affect about 5% of Canada's exports to the US.

Prime Minister Carney suspended negotiations and said Canada would match the tariffs dollar for dollar from 8 September, targeting steel, dairy, appliances, farm equipment, paper and electronics. US Trade Representative Greer blamed Canada for backing out of agreed terms. Carney said the escalation calls the continental trade pact into question.

What it means: Manufacturers, retailers and logistics firms must re-plan sourcing and pricing. Supply-chain, scenario and market-diversification work is in demand.

What we don't know yet

  • Whether talks resume after the US midterms.
  • Net effect on consumer prices in both countries.
