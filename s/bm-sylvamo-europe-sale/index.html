Sylvamo proposes sale of its Europe paper business to Midas Atlantic Partners

Confirmed · 2 min

Open in the full site →

According to Sylvamo's 6 November release, the proposed sale covers its uncoated freesheet mills in Saillat, France and Nymolla, Sweden, plus forestry and supporting operations. The buyer would take the business with an agreed level of working capital and cash, and Sylvamo would keep the right to 20% of future distributions. A definitive share purchase agreement follows works-council consultation.

The release quotes CEO John Sims describing it as an important strategic step that will leave Sylvamo more focused. Finviz's earnings coverage reports the European business is already shown as discontinued operations and that shares rose about 12% on the day.

Neither source discloses the price.

What it means: Carve-out and separation planning, works-council consultation process support and post-sale refocus on Latin America and North America.

What we don't know yet

  • Deal value and Europe headcount were not disclosed
  • Outcome of works-council consultations will determine whether the sale proceeds
