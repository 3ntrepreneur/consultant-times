Cisco posts record $18.5 billion quarter and raises FY27 guidance on AI networking demand

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Cisco reported first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $18.5 billion, up 24% year on year, with networking product orders up 28%, according to Fierce Network and Constellation Research. Non-GAAP EPS was $1.40, seven cents above Wall Street estimates, per Constellation.

Cisco guided fiscal 2027 revenue to $74.8-75.8 billion and non-GAAP EPS to $5.47-5.53, against consensus of about $73.2 billion and $5.15, according to Constellation. Fierce Network reports that hyperscaler AI infrastructure revenue is expected to reach about $9 billion in fiscal 2027, more than double fiscal 2026. Orders excluding hyperscalers rose 14%, the outlet says, and CEO Chuck Robbins described a multi-year networking supercycle. CFO Mark Patterson said optics is the tightest part of the supply chain, per the same report.

What it means: AI data-centre and network-modernisation programmes for enterprises and telcos, supply-chain sourcing work around optics, and vendor-selection support as networking spend recovers.

What we don't know yet

  • How much of the growth reaches enterprise buyers rather than hyperscalers and service providers
  • Share-price reaction was not confirmed
