Sherritt temporarily lays off 280 workers as refining stays halted

Confirmed · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Sherritt International said on 4 November 2026 that it will temporarily lay off about 280 employees, or 47% of its Canadian workforce, in a release distributed by Business Wire. The layoffs are expected to last about 90 days, and staff may be recalled earlier. Interim chief executive Peter Hancock said the layoffs are intended to be temporary.

The company says refining at its Fort Saskatchewan facility stopped on 22 June 2026 when feed inventory ran out, while ammonia fertiliser and sulphuric acid production continues. Sherritt suspended its direct participation in Cuba joint-venture activity on 7 May 2026, citing U.S. Executive Order 14404, and says it has operated under constrained liquidity conditions. It received its first emergency U.S. Treasury OFAC licence on 19 October 2026.

The release does not say when refining will restart, how remaining regulatory licences will be handled, or where its recapitalisation transaction stands. It also notes that the company's chief human resources officer has left.

What it means: Turnaround and liquidity planning, sanctions-licence and supply-chain contingency work, and recapitalisation advisory for a Canadian metals refiner cut off from Cuban feed.
