America's Car-Mart files Chapter 11 with a stalking-horse sale

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

America's Car-Mart filed Chapter 11 on 24 November 2026 in the Southern District of Texas, listing assets of $100 million to $500 million and liabilities of $1 billion to $10 billion, according to Bloomberg Law and TheStreet.

TheStreet reports a proposed stalking-horse sale to Cardinal Asset Holdings and up to $60 million of new debtor-in-possession loans, both subject to court approval. Dealerships are expected to stay open.

What it means: Restructuring, DIP financing, receivables valuation and turnaround work; credit-risk reviews for other subprime auto lenders.
