Toyota relaunches Scion and brings its KEI-01 mini truck to the US in 2027

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Toyota Motor North America presented the Scion by Toyota brand and the KEI-01 on 9 November, with a press release dated 10 November, according to a distributed Toyota release. The KEI-01 is a kei-style truck with a 660cc three-cylinder engine, selectable four-wheel drive, a top speed of 25 mph and design aimed at federal low-speed-vehicle rules.

Initial sales will be a fleet-focused pilot through selected dealers from the existing Toyota network, with limited retail possible later depending on market conditions, according to the release and the Honolulu Star-Advertiser. Assembly will take place at Toyota Motor Manufacturing Indiana from the second half of 2027, with a $34 million investment expected to add about 50 jobs.

Toyota describes Scion as a "freedom lab" for new mobility ideas and also showed side-by-side and compact personal-mobility concepts, per the release. Neither source gave a price or volume target.

What it means: Go-to-market, dealer-network and fleet-channel strategy for a new vehicle category; regulatory and low-speed-vehicle compliance work; business-case and pricing studies for niche mobility products.
