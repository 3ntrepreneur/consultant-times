Deloitte: 87% of US consumers plan to shop Black Friday-Cyber Monday, a 10-year high

Confirmed · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Deloitte said on 23 November 2026 that 87% of US consumers plan to take part in Black Friday and Cyber Monday events, up from 82% in 2025 and the highest share since 2016. The figures come from a survey of 1,200 consumers conducted between 19 and 26 October 2026.

Average planned spend is $624, flat on last year, and 72% plan to delay some purchases until the sales. Households earning $100,000 to $199,000 plan to spend 7% less, while households under $50,000 plan to spend 17% more. Gen Z expects to spend $752, up 23%, and 94% of that group plan to take part.

On channels, budgets split 61% online and 39% in store; 55% plan to shop online on Black Friday, up seven points, and 53% in store, up four. Deloitte also reports that 47% say rising shipping costs may push them toward stores or pickup. The release is the firm's own survey, and the sample is small for a national forecast.

What it means: Retailers and consumer-goods clients will ask for promotion-planning, pricing and omnichannel fulfilment work; flat average spend with higher participation signals a deal-driven, margin-pressured season.

What we don't know yet

  • How actual sales compare with stated intent
