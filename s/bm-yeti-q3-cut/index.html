YETI trims its 2026 outlook as U.S. direct-to-consumer demand softens

Confirmed · 2 min

Open in the full site →

YETI reported third-quarter sales of $509.4 million on 12 November 2026, up 4%, with wholesale up 19% but direct-to-consumer down 6%, according to its earnings release. Adjusted EPS rose 49% to $0.91 on adjusted gross margin of 59%.

The company cut 2026 adjusted sales growth to about 6% from 7-8%, and adjusted EPS to $2.90-2.95 from $2.94-3.00, per the release. Stocktwits reports the shares fell about 11% premarket and quotes CEO Matt Reintjes citing uneven demand in the U.S. and Canada, weak online and Amazon channels, and a cautious consumer who could weigh on the fourth quarter. International sales rose 19% and U.S. sales 1%, the release shows. YETI also changed how it reports adjusted results, excluding recall reserve adjustments and a tariff refund benefit, so comparisons with earlier periods are not like for like.

What it means: Channel-mix and direct-to-consumer reviews, international expansion planning, and pricing and promotion strategy as a premium brand loses online momentum.
