BMO and RBC sell Moneris to Francisco Partners

Open in the full site →

The two banks, which each held 50%, agreed to sell their payments processor in an all-cash deal. They keep exclusive customer-referral arrangements. RBC expects an after-tax gain of about C$475 million and BMO about C$600 million.

What it means: Banks are separating ownership of payment infrastructure from the distribution of it. Expect more carve-outs and referral-model deals.
