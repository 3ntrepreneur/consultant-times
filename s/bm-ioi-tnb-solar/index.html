IOI and TNB form joint venture for 500 MW solar and storage project in Negeri Sembilan

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

TNB Renewables, IOI's Luminous Aspect and a consortium of 12 SMEs signed around 3 November to develop the project through Bahau Energy Ventures, according to The Star. Ownership is 70% TNB Renewables, 25% Luminous and 5% the SME consortium. The site is in Bahau, Negeri Sembilan, and the project includes a 250 MW / 1,000 MWh battery system under Malaysia's Corporate Renewable Energy Supply Scheme.

The Star says maximum equity is RM606.2 million, with TNB's share up to RM424.4 million, and funding will combine equity with project debt at up to 80:20. DayOne Data Centres is expected to buy up to 500 MWac over 21 years. Enerdata, citing an IOI release, reports the same structure and that IOI will lease the site.

TNB said the deal is not expected to materially affect its earnings per share or gearing this year, according to The Star. No construction or operation dates were given.
