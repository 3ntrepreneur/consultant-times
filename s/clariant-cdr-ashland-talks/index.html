Clariant and CD&R confirm talks to buy Ashland

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Clariant confirmed on Monday 23 November 2026 that it is in discussions about a possible acquisition of Ashland in partnership with private equity firm Clayton, Dubilier & Rice (CD&R). In an ad hoc announcement issued from Muttenz, Switzerland, the company said it was responding to market speculation. It disclosed no price or terms and said there is no certainty that a transaction will be agreed or completed.

Reuters reported that Bloomberg News had said on Sunday that the two were negotiating an all-cash deal, and gave a value of about $3.39 billion, close to Ashland's recent trading price. Ashland's market capitalisation is $3.32 billion according to LSEG data cited by Reuters, and its shares rose about 1% in extended trading.

Reuters adds that Ashland has been exploring a sale since July after activist investors argued the company was undervalued, and that several private equity firms have shown interest. Clariant's own release says it will make a further announcement if and when appropriate. No advisers are named in either source.

For scale, Clariant's release states that it had 10,281 employees and sales of CHF 3.915 billion in its 2025 financial year. The unknowns are the structure of the Clariant and CD&R partnership, who would own which assets, the price, and whether rival bidders emerge.

What it means: Due diligence, synergy sizing and portfolio-review work for the bidders, separation and carve-out planning if assets are sold on, and defence and strategic-alternatives work for Ashland's board.

What we don't know yet

  • Price, structure and the split of assets between Clariant and CD&R
  • Whether other private equity bidders remain in the process
  • Regulatory path and timing
