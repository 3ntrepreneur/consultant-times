Nscale updates its NYSE IPO filing, showing revenue up 14-fold and a $1.69 billion loss

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Nscale's S-1/A, filed on 12 November for a planned NYSE listing, reports revenue of $307.5 million for the nine months to 30 September, against $21.3 million a year earlier, and a net loss of $1,691.9 million versus $433.3 million, per Let's Data Science. Adjusted EBITDA was minus $289.5 million. The outlet notes CNBC described these as quarterly figures when the filing presents nine- and six-month totals.

Neither source gives offer terms: the share count and price range have not been set, according to Let's Data Science and Fierce Network. Fierce describes Nscale as a UK neocloud spun out of Arkon Energy with infrastructure and cloud units, and says a $103 billion figure refers to total contracted value rather than valuation.

Let's Data Science adds that Microsoft and Anthropic are expected to be significant customers, and cites reports that Microsoft walked away from a planned English site over electricity access. Renaissance Capital's earlier coverage, which this run did not open, was returned in search for the original filing.

What it means: IPO readiness, equity-story and investor-communications work for AI infrastructure firms; diligence on contracted-backlog quality, customer concentration and power-access risk.

What we don't know yet
