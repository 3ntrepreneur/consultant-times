TDCX opens its sixth China centre in Foshan with about 100 initial roles, serving the Greater Bay Area (Consultancy.asia).

Singapore-based customer-experience and outsourcing group TDCX has opened a campus in Foshan, its sixth centre in China, according to Consultancy.asia on 25 November 2026. The site offers Cantonese and Mandarin service and starts with about 100 positions, with headcount expected to grow quickly over two years.

The report quotes Tony Chen, TDCX's China country director, saying a Cantonese or Mandarin pod can be set up in 60 to 90 days. It describes TDCX as founded in 1995, with more than 20,000 employees across 37 locations.

The move shows outsourcers adding capacity close to Greater Bay Area clients. Contract values and client names were not given.
