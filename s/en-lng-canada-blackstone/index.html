Blackstone and four Canadian pension funds buy into LNG Canada through Mitsubishi's stake

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Blackstone Credit and Insurance, with CPP Investments, OMERS, IMCO and PSP, signed the deal on 2 November. Mitsubishi stays the controlling partner and keeps its LNG offtake, about 2.1 million tonnes a year now and 4.2 million after expansion. Reuters reports Mitsubishi values the interest being sold at about US$1.6 billion, with the rest of the investment aimed at Phase 2.

LNG Canada, led by Shell with PetroChina, Petronas, Mitsubishi and KOGAS as partners, began production in June 2025. The partners approved Phase 2 in September 2026, which lifts capacity from roughly 14 to 28 million tonnes a year. Mitsubishi says the deal is expected to close in November.

The structure is the story: a trading house sells a minority stake in a holding company to recycle capital while keeping control and its gas. It is a template other Asian buyers with large project stakes could follow.

What it means: Infrastructure capital is now financing LNG build-out alongside the majors. Expect work on partner capital structures, offtake contracting and construction-cost control as Phase 2 proceeds.
