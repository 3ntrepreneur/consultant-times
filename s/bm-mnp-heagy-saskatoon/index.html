MNP to merge with Saskatoon firm Heagy LLP

Confirmed · 3 min

Open in the full site →

MNP announced on 5 November 2026 that Heagy LLP, a Saskatoon firm focused on assurance, tax and business advisory, will join MNP effective 4 January 2027. Four Heagy partners, Richard Matchett, James Schemenauer, Alan Ashdown and Scott Ehrmantraut, will become MNP partners, supported by 14 team members.

The Heagy team stays at its current location, which becomes MNP's second Saskatoon office. MNP says it has more than 860 team members, including 142 partners, in Saskatchewan, and completed a merger with Green Finance earlier this year.

What it means: Integration and succession work in mid-market accounting; shows Canadian firms still consolidating regional practices.
