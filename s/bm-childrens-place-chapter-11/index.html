Children's Place files Chapter 11 and seeks to sell its assets

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

The Children's Place filed Chapter 11 petitions in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of New Jersey, according to a company release cited by Bloomberg Law on 9 November 2026. The retailer says it intends to sell substantially all of its assets and expects the sale process to finish by the end of December 2026. Bids are due on 9 December.

The company says it is talking with multiple parties about transactions that would let the business keep operating, and plans a new term loan from its existing secured lenders. Its stores and e-commerce sites remain open during the process, according to the report.

The readable part of the article does not give debt totals, store counts or the reasons for the filing, so those details are unknown here.
