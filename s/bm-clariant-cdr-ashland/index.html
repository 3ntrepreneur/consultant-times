Clariant and CD&R confirm talks to acquire Ashland

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Clariant said on 23 November 2026 that it is in discussions about a potential acquisition of Ashland in partnership with Clayton, Dubilier & Rice, responding to market speculation. It gave no price and said no transaction is certain.

Reuters says Ashland has explored a sale since July after activist investors argued it was undervalued, and that several private equity firms have shown interest.

What it means: Due diligence, synergy sizing and separation planning for the bidders; strategic-alternatives support for Ashland.

What we don't know yet

  • Price and structure
  • Whether rival bidders remain
