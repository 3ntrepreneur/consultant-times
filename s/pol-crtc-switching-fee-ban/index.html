CRTC bans fees that stand in the way of switching phone and internet plans

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Telecom Regulatory Policy CRTC 2026-43, issued 12 March, prohibits fees tied to activating, changing or cancelling a cellphone or internet plan. RefDesk reports the policy carves out narrow exceptions for physical installation and explicitly chosen additional products. The ban took effect 12 June 2026.

The CRTC has since opened a show-cause proceeding on new device, SIM and shipping fees. Rogers and Telus both blame the ban for lower fee revenue in their third-quarter results.

What it means: Carriers' wireless pricing, device and onboarding models need redesign. Competitors and fintech-style resellers gain room.

What we don't know yet

  • Final penalties in the show-cause case.
  • How narrowly the CRTC will read the 'optional service' exception.
