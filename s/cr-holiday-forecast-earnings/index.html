Retailers report into a forecast of a $1 trillion holiday season, the fastest growth since 2021

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

NRF released the forecast on 15 October, built with Oxford Economics. Last year holiday sales rose 3.1% to $972 billion, below NRF's own range. It says nearly half of this year's growth is real spending and the rest is higher prices, and that growth would be the fastest since 2021.

Earnings land in a tight window: Home Depot on 17 November, Target on 18 November, Walmart and Ross on 19 November and Best Buy on 24 November. Lowe's lists 18 November as tentative.

The forecast is nominal and covers retail excluding autos, gas stations and restaurants, so it will not map neatly onto any single company's results.

What it means: Retail clients will be re-planning inventory, pricing and promotions in real time. Watch guidance language on discretionary categories and low-income shoppers.

What we don't know yet

  • Company guidance for the holiday quarter.
  • Earnings dates other than those the companies have confirmed.
