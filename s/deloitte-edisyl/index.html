Deloitte spins up a new AI company, Edisyl

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Deloitte has bought its way into another AI business. Its venture arm, D71 Ventures, has acquired the AI business of Flipside Crypto and relaunched it as Edisyl, a new standalone enterprise AI company, Consultancy.org reported on November 13.

Flipside is a Boston-based firm best known for blockchain data and analytics tools for crypto organisations. According to CB Insights, it raised about $65.7 million over seven funding rounds and was valued at $350 million in April 2022.

The deal fits a broader push at Deloitte. Its US arm launched Zora AI, a suite of AI agents, and the firm then set up a Global Agentic Network to build agents for clients. Bloomberg Law reported in September that Deloitte is almost halfway through a five-year commitment to spend $3 billion on AI.

What it means: Rival firms' boards will ask whether they should buy AI companies too, and acquired AI businesses need integration and go-to-market help.

What we don't know yet

  • What Deloitte paid. No price has been reported.
  • How many Flipside staff are moving to Edisyl.
  • What Edisyl will sell, and whether Deloitte audit clients will be allowed to buy it.
