EY launches EY.ai Academy across the Middle East and North Africa

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

EY has launched the EY.ai Academy across the Middle East and North Africa, Consultancy-me.com reported on 19 November 2026. The programme offers AI-focused learning and development for organisations in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Egypt and other Middle Eastern markets.

According to the report, learning paths are tailored for leaders, managers, technical specialists and frontline teams. The curriculum covers AI foundations, responsible AI and governance, data foundations, AI and machine learning platforms, and how to identify, prioritise and activate business use cases. Separate streams address organisational culture, leadership commitment, change management and adoption measurement.

Delivery includes e-learning, executive sessions, workshops, bootcamps and peer learning in Arabic and English. The programmes can be bought on their own or as part of wider transformation work by EY consultants, and are tailored by sector and workforce. EY says the Academy has supported more than 200,000 learners worldwide; that is EY's figure.

Mazen Baroudi, EY's MENA chief technology officer, said the region is entering a defining phase of AI adoption. Omar Odeh, MENA consulting leader, and Wasfi Masri, people consulting leader, are also named.

No price, client list or enrolment target for the region was given. For the industry, the signal is that adoption, not tooling, is where firms expect the next wave of paid work, and that training is a way into larger programmes.

What it means: AI upskilling, change-management and adoption-measurement work for regional enterprises and governments; a sign that training is being bundled with consulting engagements.
