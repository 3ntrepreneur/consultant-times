Health and personal care is the fastest-growing retail category in Canada this year

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Retail Insider's September monitor, built on Statistics Canada data, says health and personal care retailers saw sales rise 12.2% year over year in July and volumes rise 10.7%. Its June commodity data show home health products up 15.9% and infant care, personal and beauty products up 9.1%. The publication says the pattern has held through 2026.

Trading Economics' summary of the advance September estimate also lists health and personal care retailers among the gainers, up 1.6% on the month.

What it means: Pharmacy, beauty and wellness retail is outgrowing the rest of the market, which favours chains with pharmacy and loyalty assets.
