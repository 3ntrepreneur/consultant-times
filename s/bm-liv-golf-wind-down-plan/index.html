LIV Golf files Chapter 11 plan for orderly wind-down

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

LIV Golf filed a Chapter 11 plan in New Jersey on 10 November 2026 setting out an orderly wind-down, according to the Associated Press. The league entered bankruptcy on 8 September after Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund stopped funding it following the 2026 season. A restructuring deal with BC Partners Credit, which had pledged $300 million, did not win enough support to create a new version of the league, the AP reports.

The filing says the Public Investment Fund put in about $5 billion of equity, the AP reports. It also says 289 employees were laid off and 22 furloughed about a week before the filing, and that $15.6 million in retention bonuses went to seven executives. LIV's own summary says no viable restructuring transaction remains, according to the report.

The plan would route proceeds to creditors through a wind-down trust. The AP's account of court hearing dates is unclear, so the schedule should be checked against the court docket. Creditor claim totals and the final liquidation timeline are not stated in the article.

What it means: Wind-down and liquidation-trust advisory, creditor and contract unwinding, and sports-media rights repricing as a major league funder exits.
