LNG Canada Phase 2 gets the green light, doubling Kitimat to 28 million tonnes a year

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Shell Canada Energy announced a final investment decision on LNG Canada Phase 2 on 29 September. It adds two trains, lifting capacity at Kitimat, B.C., from 14 to 28 million tonnes a year, plus storage, a loading berth and five new Coastal GasLink compressor stations. Shell holds 40%, with Petronas, PetroChina, Mitsubishi and Korea Gas as partners.

The Hub reports Ottawa values the project at $32 billion of private investment and that it moved from federal referral to decision in twelve months. Fluor and JGC's joint venture received notice to proceed.

What it means: Engineering, procurement, gas-supply and port clients get a long project pipeline. Policy makers get a test case for faster approvals.

What we don't know yet

  • The $32 billion is Ottawa's valuation, not a company-disclosed capex figure.
