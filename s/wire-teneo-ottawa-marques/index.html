Teneo hires Elder Marques, ex-Blakes partner and finance-minister chief of staff, to open its Ottawa office (Consulting.ca).

Reported

Open in the full site →

Teneo has hired Elder Marques as a senior managing director to set up its Ottawa office and build its public affairs work in Ottawa and across Canada, according to Consulting.ca on 25 November 2026. The report says he has more than 20 years of legal and public-sector experience, including about five years as a partner at Blake, Cassels & Graydon and roles as chief of staff to two federal ministers.

Teneo has offices in Toronto, Montreal and Calgary with about 60 professionals in Canada, according to the report, and it hired Phil Reynolds on 10 November to lead a new Canadian financial advisory practice. James Crossland, Teneo's global vice chairman, leads the Canadian business. The hire points to demand for government-relations and regulatory advice among Canadian clients.
