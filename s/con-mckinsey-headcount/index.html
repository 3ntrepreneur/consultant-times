McKinsey has shrunk from a 45,000 peak and weighs cutting about 10% of non-client roles

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

StrategyCase, written by a former McKinsey senior consultant, says McKinsey took its headcount from a 2022 peak above 45,000 to roughly 40,000 while planning a 12% increase in North American headcount in 2026, with junior ranks up as much as 20%. Quartz notes revenue growth has stalled after the multi-year hiring spree.

AI Weekly summarizes reports that McKinsey is targeting 10% cuts in non-client-facing headcount over 18 to 24 months, and that PwC US cut about 600 executive-assistant roles. A McKinsey spokesperson said the aim is to make support functions more efficient, including by using AI.

What it means: The 'barbell' is the story: entry-level and senior roles hold, and the middle and back office shrink. It matters for anyone planning a consulting career.

What we don't know yet

  • The cut size is reported, not announced; McKinsey has not published a number.
