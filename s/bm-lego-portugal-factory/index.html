LEGO picks Portugal for a €1 billion factory, advised by Buck Consultants

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

LEGO has chosen Estarreja, in Portugal's Aveiro district, for a new €1 billion factory expected to create more than 1,000 jobs, according to Consultancy.eu on 25 November 2026. The site covers 60 hectares and could grow to 116; the plant is 115,000 square metres and will handle moulding, processing, packing and warehousing. Construction is expected to start in the first half of 2027, with operations in 2030.

Buck Consultants International advised on location strategy and site selection, a process Consultancy.eu says took about a year. Portuguese newspaper Observador, cited by the report, says Portugal beat at least Spain and Poland. Portugal's trade agency AICEP began talks about a year earlier, and the Estarreja municipality joined in February 2026.

It would be LEGO's fourth European factory and eighth worldwide, after Billund, Kladno and Nyíregyháza in Europe. The report also lists a Vietnam plant opened in April 2025 and a US plant planned for 2027. LEGO targets 100% renewable energy, about 80% of it generated on site, and LEED Platinum certification, according to the report.

For consultants the signal is the mix of work around a plant of this size: site selection and incentive negotiation, supply-chain network design, energy and sustainability engineering, and workforce planning in a region where more than 1,000 hires are needed.

What it means: Site selection, incentive negotiation, supply-chain network design and workforce planning for manufacturers weighing new plants; Buck Consultants International is named as LEGO's adviser.
