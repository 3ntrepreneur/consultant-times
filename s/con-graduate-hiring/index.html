Graduate hiring splits: Bain raises its intake 25% while the Big Four trim theirs

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

City A.M., via Pulse, reports Bain raised graduate hires 25% to exceed pre-pandemic levels, Alvarez & Marsal is targeting US and European campuses and BCG is holding or slightly raising entry-level hiring. The same report says the Big Four have already cut hundreds of graduate roles.

The Irish Times reported in December that McKinsey, BCG and Bain froze starting salaries for 2026 for a third year, and that Big Four starting pay has not risen since 2022. In February the Irish Times reported PwC UK applications up 35% and that PwC chose not to automate some junior work so staff learn judgment.

What it means: For a first-year student, the signal is that AI fluency and judgment matter more than volume of entry roles. Firms are choosing different strategies.

What we don't know yet
