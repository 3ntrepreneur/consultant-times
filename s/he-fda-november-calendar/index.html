November's FDA calendar: a dozen-plus decisions, from a lung-cancer antibody to Duchenne cell therapy

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

RTTNews counts more than a dozen PDUFA target dates in November. Trackers list Summit Therapeutics' ivonescimab with chemotherapy on 14 November, Capricor's deramiocel for Duchenne muscular dystrophy on 22 November (its date was extended from August), Sanofi's venglustat for type 3 Gaucher disease on 25 November, and Vertex's povetacicept for IgA nephropathy on 30 November.

Dates are targets, not guarantees: the FDA can act early, extend a review or issue a rejection. Prime Therapeutics, which advises pharmacy benefit clients, also flags Bayer's asundexian for secondary stroke prevention as under priority review this month. It would be the first Factor XIa inhibitor available in the US if approved.

Gilead and Arcellx's anito-cel for relapsed multiple myeloma has a 23 December target, and Takeda's zasocitinib is expected in the first quarter of 2027.

What it means: Biotech investors, pharma business-development teams and launch consultants plan around these dates. A miss or delay moves valuations and commercial timelines.

What we don't know yet

  • Several dates are 'expected' and were not confirmed on the day.
  • Outcomes for 14 November were not available at publication.
