Canada's two big railways both grow revenue 15% in the third quarter, with volumes up 4% at CN and 6% at CPKC

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

CPKC reported third-quarter revenue of $4.2 billion, up 15%, on a 6% increase in revenue ton-miles, with core adjusted earnings per share of $1.28, up 16%. Reported operating ratio rose 130 basis points to 64.8%, while the core adjusted ratio was 61.9%. Its CEO said the North American network countered macro headwinds.

Railway Age reports CN's third-quarter revenue was C$4.771 billion, up 15%, with revenue ton-miles up 4% and nine-month free cash flow of C$2.918 billion, up 25%. CN still projects low single-digit revenue ton-mile growth for 2026 and mid-to-high single-digit adjusted EPS growth, tracking toward the top of that range.

What it means: Rail volumes are a real-economy signal. Shippers should expect continued network competition and rate discipline.

What we don't know yet

  • Quarterly volume data does not show whether tariff effects are building.
