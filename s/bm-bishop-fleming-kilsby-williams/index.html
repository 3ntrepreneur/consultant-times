Bishop Fleming buys Kilsby Williams, entering South Wales

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Bishop Fleming, a UK audit, accountancy, tax and advisory firm, has acquired Kilsby Williams, an independent accounting and advisory practice in South Wales, according to Consultancy.uk on 20 November 2026. The deal adds around 80 staff and gives Bishop Fleming its first presence in the region, taking it to ten offices across the West Midlands, South West and Wales.

Kilsby Williams was founded in 1991 and reported revenue of about £7.5 million in its latest financial year. It will keep operating from its Newport office, initially under its own name, while integration continues. Bishop Fleming says the combined firm has well over 600 people and approaches 70 partners.

The article describes this as Bishop Fleming's first acquisition since a minority investment from private equity investor Synova in 2025. Andrew Sandiford, Bishop Fleming's chief executive and managing partner, called it an important moment for the firm. Simon Tee, Kilsby Williams' managing partner, said the firm can keep what clients value while gaining scale and investment. Price, closing date and any integration timetable were not stated.
