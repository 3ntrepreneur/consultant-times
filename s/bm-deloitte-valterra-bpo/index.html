Deloitte wins Valterra Platinum finance, HR and payroll outsourcing

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Valterra Platinum, the Johannesburg-based miner formerly known as Anglo American Platinum, has signed a business process outsourcing agreement with Deloitte, according to Consultancy.co.za on 20 November 2026. Deloitte will take over corporate support functions including finance, HR and payroll through its Finance and HR Operate practice.

More than 130 Valterra employees are moving to Deloitte's South African operations, the article says, and the appointment followed a competitive process. Valterra became an independent company in 2025.

Ruwayda Redfearn, chief executive of Deloitte's Africa business unit, is quoted saying the deal advances its ambition to scale outsourcing; the article cites a goal of more than 30% of revenue from outsource services. Valterra chief executive Craig Miller calls it an important step.

What it means: Outsourcing and transition work for miners separating from a parent; shows Big Four firms pushing operate services beyond advice, with Deloitte targeting 30%+ of revenue from outsourcing.
