FDA's October: a first antisense drug for hepatitis B and a rulebook for drug-companion apps

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

RTTNews says the FDA approved GSK's Hibsago (bepirovirsen) on 20 October, in combination with existing antivirals, for some adults with chronic hepatitis B without cirrhosis. It is described as the first approved antisense oligonucleotide for the disease.

The same day the FDA issued final guidance on apps that drug makers develop, distribute and label alongside prescription medicines. It sets out when app content falls under submission requirements and when an app should be named on a drug's label.

Epocrates' October roundup lists other notable approvals, including Awiqli as the first once-weekly basal insulin for children aged 10 and over with type 2 diabetes, and a Lucentis biosimilar, Lucamzi.

What it means: Pharma commercial and digital teams get a defined path for companion apps. Hepatitis B treatment gets a new class.
