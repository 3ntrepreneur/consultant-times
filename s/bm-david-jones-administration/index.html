Australia's David Jones enters administration as a sale process begins

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

The board of the 188-year-old retailer appointed McGrathNicol as voluntary administrator, while lender Hilco Capital appointed FTI Consulting's Christopher Hill as receiver, according to HCAmag. The business has 38 stores across Australia and New Zealand and more than 6,000 staff, and stores continue to trade during the process, per both outlets.

Scentre Group, its largest landlord, said rent had not been paid since 30 September, according to HCAmag. Nation Thailand, citing ABC News, says Scentre offered financial support that owner Anchorage Capital Partners rejected. Before the collapse the retailer had sought three months of rent relief and a footprint of about 12 key stores, per HCAmag.

The administrators are seeking a capital injection or a sale as a going concern, Nation Thailand reports. Neither outlet gave a total debt figure, and store closures and job cuts were expected to be updated later.

What it means: Turnaround, store-footprint and lease renegotiation work; sale-readiness and carve-out preparation; landlord and lender restructuring advisory for department-store operators.
