Bain expects US Black Friday-to-Cyber Monday sales above $85 billion

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Bain & Company's forecast says US retail sales from Black Friday through Cyber Monday should top $85 billion this year, about 8.5% of total holiday spending. It expects growth of 8.75% over 2025 in the window, against 4.5% for the full holiday season.

A separate eMarketer forecast, reported by Talk Business, predicts US holiday retail sales growth of 4.1%, with Black Friday e-commerce at $13.4 billion, up 7.7%. The forecasts rest on assumptions about gas prices, inflation and tariffs.

What it means: Retail share of year is moving into fewer, bigger days. It affects staffing, fulfilment and media-spend planning for clients with US exposure.
