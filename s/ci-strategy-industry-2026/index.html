Strategy firms keep growing but not evenly: BCG up 7%, McKinsey's headcount shrinks, Accenture adds people

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

An industry review notes only BCG discloses revenue among the strategy firms, so comparisons rely on estimates. Revenue kept growing through 2025 across the big firms, but McKinsey's headcount falling was a break from the pattern.

Accenture is often grouped with the Big Four but is not one of them. It reported about 814,000 people at the end of fiscal 2026 versus about 779,000 a year earlier, and Deloitte says it has nearly 500,000. At the Big Four, tax grew faster than advisory: PwC's advisory revenue rose 3.0% like-for-like versus 6.3% for tax and legal.

The same review says Bain bought a London financial-crime compliance firm, JJC FinTech, in February 2026.

What it means: Firm leaders are re-cutting pyramids and pricing. For students and early-career candidates, the entry-level squeeze is the headline.
