Drax, Brookfield and Radiant plan £20bn, 1GW UK data centre

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Drax, Brookfield and Brookfield's neocloud platform Radiant have agreed a £20 billion partnership (about US$26.8 billion) to deliver up to 1GW of UK data-centre capacity, according to Construction Briefing on 3 November 2026. The site is Drax Power Station near Selby in North Yorkshire.

The first 100MW phase could receive up to £2.5 billion of backing from the Brookfield-Qai AI infrastructure joint venture, the article says, and could be running from the first quarter of 2028. Capacity could then ramp to 500MW between 2028 and 2031. Phase one depends on planning, regulatory and grid approvals and on finding a tenant.

Drax chief executive Will Gardiner said the company is proud to partner with leading AI infrastructure providers on the project. The tenant and final investment decision are not yet known, which is where most of the delivery risk sits.

What it means: Site, grid-connection and power-sourcing strategy, planning and regulatory approvals, and tenant-sourcing work for AI data-centre developments on power-station sites.

What we don't know yet

  • Tenant and final investment decision not announced
  • Planning, regulatory and grid approvals still required
