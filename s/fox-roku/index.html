Fox shareholders and Roku holders approve the $160-a-share takeover

Open in the full site →

Fox is paying $96 in cash and 0.9693 Fox Class A shares per Roku share. The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027 but faces a second DOJ request for information. Fox expects about $400 million of run-rate cost savings.

What it means: A broadcaster buying a streaming platform. Expect work on integration, ad-tech and distribution-terms negotiations.
