Fiserv discloses 3,000 job cuts and doubles Project Elevate savings target to $1 billion

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

According to People Matters, Fiserv disclosed on 3 November that roughly 3,000 employee and contractor positions have gone since June and that senior management was reduced by more than 10%. The company had more than 38,000 employees at the end of 2025, per the same report. AI Weekly, citing PYMNTS, says management framed the programme as an AI-everywhere reorganisation, with fraud review, customer service and software work slated for automation.

Fiserv's results release confirms the Project Elevate target rose to $1 billion from $500 million after a portfolio review, and reports $83 million of severance in the quarter plus a $6.45 billion goodwill impairment. People Matters reports Fiserv also plans to exit more than a dozen smaller products and that the new target sits below the $1.25 billion activist Jana Partners had proposed; the release itself did not mention product exits.

Both trade outlets report shares rising about 9.5% premarket.

What it means: Operating-model redesign, AI-enabled cost take-out, product-portfolio rationalisation and management-layer reductions at a payments processor under activist scrutiny.

What we don't know yet

  • Which divisions bore the cuts and the period for the $1B savings were not disclosed
  • The results release gave no headcount figures; the 3,000 figure comes from the call as reported
