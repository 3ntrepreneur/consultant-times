Trans Mountain plans to stretch capacity to 1.25 million barrels a day

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Energy Now, carrying Bloomberg, reports Trans Mountain plans to raise capacity from 890,000 to 1.25 million barrels a day over four or five years using drag-reducing agents and extra pumping capacity. Since the expansion started up in May 2024, Canadian heavy crude has traded at an average discount of about $12 a barrel to the US benchmark, against almost $17 a barrel the year before.

Transport Topics, also via Bloomberg, says Enbridge reached a final investment decision in November 2025 to add 150,000 barrels a day to the Mainline and 100,000 to Flanagan South at a cost of about $1.4 billion.

What it means: Marketers and producers should plan around incremental, not new-build, capacity until a new line is approved.
