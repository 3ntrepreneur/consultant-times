Anthropic opens 34% above offer in first frontier-AI listing

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Anthropic's shares opened 34.3% above their offer price on its Nasdaq debut on Thursday 19 November 2026, according to a Reuters factbox. The factbox calls it the first listing of a frontier AI lab and the second multibillion-dollar IPO this year by a company valued above $1.5 trillion, after SpaceX in June. It does not give Anthropic's offer price.

Reuters lists how other major 2026 US listings opened. Cerebras opened 89% above its $185 offer price in May and raised $5.55 billion. SpaceX opened 11.1% above its $135 offer price in June and raised $75 billion. Bending Spoons opened about 7% above its $29 offer price in July, raising $1.68 billion with selling shareholders. Jersey Mike's opened 8.7% below its $23 offer price in July, raising about $1 billion. vVardis opened 22.2% below its $18 offer price in its November NYSE debut, raising about $175 million.

The spread, from an 89% pop to a 22% drop, shows the window is open but selective. Opening-day moves are not a measure of lasting value, and this factbox does not say how any of the stocks have traded since.
