Ottawa details $600 million in media funding for 2026–27; critics question who pays

Developing · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

On 8 November the government set out the 2026–27 allocation: Canada Media Fund $213.4 million, the Independent Local News Fund $80 million, Telefilm Canada $68.5 million and the Canada Music Fund $67 million. It is separate from a proposed journalism tax-credit expansion in Budget 2026.

Law professor Michael Geist argued taxpayers are covering costs that large technology platforms should bear, citing roughly $1.5 billion across several cultural and news measures. That figure is his tally of wider measures, not payments under this package.
