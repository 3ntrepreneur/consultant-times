Alexander Haas promotes Jaci Thiede to president and CEO from 1 January 2027, succeeding David King, who stays as senior partner.

Reported

Alexander Haas, an Atlanta-based fundraising consultancy for nonprofits, has promoted Jaci Thiede to president and chief executive effective 1 January 2027, according to Consulting.us on 18 November 2026. She succeeds David King, who led the firm for more than 20 years and remains as a senior partner.

The firm was founded in 1987 and says it has advised more than 2,000 clients. It was acquired in 2024 by Collegium, a New York holding company for nonprofit-sector consultancies. Thiede, based in Denver, rejoined the firm as a partner in 2023 after starting her career there in 1994, and is its third leader and first woman in the role.

Collegium's founder and chief executive, Craig Leach, backed the choice in a statement. The report gives no detail on strategy changes.
