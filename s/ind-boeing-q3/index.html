Boeing's commercial unit still loses money, but the loss narrows to $697 million

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Boeing reported third-quarter revenue of $24.2 billion, up 4%, with a GAAP loss of $0.26 a share and a core loss of $0.34. Commercial Airplanes lost $697 million on $10.7 billion of revenue, a margin of negative 6.5%, versus a $5.35 billion loss a year earlier when a 777X charge hit. The unit delivered 161 aircraft and booked 300 net orders.

Defense, Space & Security earned $492 million and Global Services $1.02 billion. The 737 is still working toward 47 a month, and the 777-9 first delivery remains planned for 2027. Total backlog is a record $745 billion.

What it means: Suppliers, airlines and lessors plan around slow rate recovery. Boeing's supply-chain cost issues are an operations-improvement case.
