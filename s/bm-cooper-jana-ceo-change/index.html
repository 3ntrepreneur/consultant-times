Cooper Companies replaces CEO Al White with executive chair Walt Rosebrough after Jana pressure

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

According to MedTech Dive, Albert White stepped down as president and CEO effective immediately after eight years in the role and will advise through April. Walter Rosebrough Jr., a director and former Steris CEO, becomes executive chair and runs day-to-day operations until a permanent CEO is named, with Russell Reynolds supporting the search. A new independent director joins in December and three directors will not stand for re-election in 2027.

MedTech Dive says Jana Partners had demanded in September that Cooper start an external CEO search, appoint a new chair, consider selling its fertility and medical device assets and engage with buyers for CooperVision. Finimize reports Cooper reaffirmed its fiscal 2026 outlook and that its shares fell almost 3% in extended trading.

Note that the announcement was on 27 October, slightly before this week's window, but the follow-through on the search and any asset review is still ahead.

What it means: Board refresh and CEO search support, portfolio review of CooperVision versus surgical assets, and turnaround planning for a medtech company under activist pressure.

What we don't know yet

  • Jana's stake size was not disclosed in the coverage read
  • Whether the board will pursue the asset sales Jana asked for is unclear
