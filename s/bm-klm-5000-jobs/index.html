KLM to cut, outsource or transfer about 5,000 jobs in cost-reset drive

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Announced on 5 November alongside Air France-KLM's third-quarter results, the plan covers about 5,000 positions, according to DutchNews.nl and Skift. Skift breaks it down as roughly 1,250 catering jobs moved to Gategroup, 800 ground-services roles shifted to third parties, 2,000 positions transferred to Air France-KLM (1,500 at contact centre Cygnific) and about 1,000 reductions from productivity gains.

Skift reports KLM is targeting an extra EUR 1 billion a year of earnings improvement, taking the total to EUR 1.4 billion, and is seeking union agreement to an 8% productivity gain by 2028 with restrained wages. DutchNews.nl says KLM expects to avoid compulsory layoffs, relying on voluntary schemes and unfilled vacancies, and that unions FNV and CNV have voiced concern.

The cuts come despite a stronger quarter: Skift reports KLM's operating profit rose to EUR 458 million.

What it means: Airline cost-base redesign, outsourcing and shared-services strategy, fleet and productivity programmes, and union-facing transformation planning.

What we don't know yet

  • Implementation timetable and the final number of outright redundancies were not given
  • Union response and negotiation outcome remain open
