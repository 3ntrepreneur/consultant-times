Canada's telcos report flat wireless revenue as fee limits and price competition bite

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

BCE's third-quarter revenue rose 2.2%, but wireless service revenue fell 1.3% and ARPU fell 1.7%. Adjusted EBITDA grew 0.9%. BCE agreed to sell 49.9% of a new wireless-tower company to La Caisse for $2.45 billion and reaffirmed 2026 guidance. Net earnings fell 84.6% against a year-earlier gain from the MLSE sale.

Rogers' service revenue and adjusted EBITDA each rose 1%, with record free cash flow of $1.4 billion. Its wireless service revenue fell 1%, partly from the fee ban. MobileSyrup reports Rogers added 17,000 mobile subscribers against 62,000 a year earlier, and Telus posted a $660 million net loss on a goodwill impairment at Telus Health.

What it means: Telecom clients are shifting from subscriber growth to cost, capital allocation and asset monetization. Wireless pricing discipline is the swing factor.
