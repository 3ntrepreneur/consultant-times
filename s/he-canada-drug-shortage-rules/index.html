Canada tightens drug-shortage rules and issues guidance on extending expiry dates

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Fasken says Health Canada expanded its authority to monitor and manage drug shortages, and manufacturers must now notify it if they have not sold a drug in Canada for twelve months. Smart & Biggar reports the regulations amending the Food and Drugs Act on shortages and discontinuation of sale were published on 17 June.

On 7 October Health Canada published a guide, GUI-0500, on extending expiration dates of marketed drugs in response to shortages.

What it means: Supply-chain, manufacturers and hospital procurement teams face new notification duties. Resilience planning becomes a compliance matter.
