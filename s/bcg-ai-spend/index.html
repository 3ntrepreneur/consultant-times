BCG: companies now spend 3.3% of revenue on AI, almost double late 2025

Reported

Open in the full site →

BCG estimates AI investment has climbed to 3.3% of revenue from 1.7% in late 2025, but payoffs stay patchy because human sign-offs remain after AI does the task.

What it means: Every CFO funding AI will ask where the payoff is: workflow redesign and ROI-tracking work.
