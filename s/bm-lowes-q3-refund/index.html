Lowe's beats on EPS, but a $250 million tariff refund flatters the quarter

Confirmed · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Lowe's reported third-quarter sales of $22.8 billion on 18 November 2026, up 9.3%, with comparable sales up 1.8%, its sixth straight positive quarter, according to its earnings release filed with the SEC. Adjusted EPS was $3.10 and online sales rose 17.4%. Gross margin fell to 32.95% from 34.19%.

The release says IEEPA tariff refunds added about $250 million before tax, or $0.34 per share. Zacks, via Finviz, calculates that excluding the refund adjusted EPS would have been about $2.76, below consensus. Full-year guidance is for sales of about $92.5 billion, comparable sales up about 0.5% and adjusted EPS of about $12.25; Zacks says the EPS figure is unchanged from August, which implies a lower underlying outlook. Shares were down about 4% premarket, per the same report. Acquisitions of Foundation Building Materials and Artisan Design Group added about 7.5 points of sales growth, according to Zacks.

What it means: Margin-bridge and gross-margin diagnostics after acquisitions, tariff-refund accounting and pricing reviews, and Pro-customer strategy work as DIY demand stays soft.

What we don't know yet

  • The release does not say whether refunds are included in full-year guidance
  • The underlying-earnings calculation is Zacks' analysis, not the company's
