Canadian manufacturing slips into contraction in October

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

S&P Global's Canada manufacturing PMI dropped to 49.6 in October from 51.5, its first contraction of 2026 and the weakest since December 2025. Production fell for the first time since March, employment declined, and selling prices rose at the fastest pace in more than four years.

Reuters says tariffs and geopolitical uncertainty weighed on activity. S&P Global's Paul Smith said the sector ran out of steam after solid growth through the year. The next reading will show whether October was a blip.

What it means: Canadian manufacturers face a split with US peers. Pricing, supply-chain and market-diversification work becomes more urgent.
