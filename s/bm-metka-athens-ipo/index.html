Metlen takes construction arm METKA public on Euronext Athens with up to €250 million of new shares

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Metlen published its intention to float on 11 November, per the London Stock Exchange RNS listing. Dnews reports up to 25 million new shares, with Metlen selling 5 million existing shares and an option for 5 million more, so the total could reach about €365 million. The indicative range is €10 to €11.50, valuing METKA at roughly €1.25 to €1.4 billion after the capital increase.

METKA's new money is earmarked, per Dnews, for up to €84 million of existing PPP and concession commitments, including a Crete highway and student housing, and €166 million for new infrastructure investments from 2027 to 2030. It reported a construction backlog of about €2.1 billion at 30 June. Metlen would keep 76% to 78% after the offer.

Dnews carried headlines saying the price was set at €10 with demand more than twice the offer; this run could not confirm them from a primary document, and the RNS body was not readable.

What it means: Carve-out and listing readiness for conglomerate units, equity-story and valuation work, and infrastructure-concession strategy for the PPP pipeline METKA says it will fund.
