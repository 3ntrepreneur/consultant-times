Gildan slashes 2026 outlook and withdraws its 2028 targets after a weak third quarter

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Gildan Activewear reported third-quarter results on 5 November 2026 and lowered its full-year outlook sharply, according to FashionUnited. Revenue guidance now stands at $5.4-5.5 billion, down from $6.0-6.2 billion, adjusted EPS at $3.70-3.80 from $4.65-4.75, and adjusted operating margin at about 20% from about 21.8%. The company also withdrew its three-year 2026-2028 targets, the outlet reports. CTV News reported that the shares fell about 25 per cent on the day.

Quarterly net sales from continuing operations were $1.47 billion, up 61.1% because of the HanesBrands integration, per FashionUnited, but wholesale revenue fell 6.8%. Management pointed to softer consumer demand, distributors cutting replenishment orders to manage working capital, and cautious inventory commitments from retail partners. Adjusted margin was helped by $177 million of tariff refunds, the report says. Gildan also sold HanesBrands Australia for about A$700 million and left net leverage at 2.7 times.

What it means: Integration re-plan and cost programmes for the HanesBrands combination, working-capital and inventory reviews with distributors, and wholesale demand forecasting work across apparel supply chains.

What we don't know yet

  • Whether the demand weakness is temporary destocking or a lasting shift was not stated
