Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns; CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO

Confirmed · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Nvidia announced on 12 November 2026 that Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately. Chief financial officer Colette Kress, who has held that role since September 2013, is interim CEO and remains CFO. The board is searching for a permanent chief executive and will consider internal and external candidates. Harvey C. Jones, a former chief executive and executive chairman of Synopsys, was appointed the company's first chair of the board.

Nvidia's announcement gives no reason for the resignation. The departure follows a board investigation that the company announced days earlier into reports that Huang had been targeted by a foreign intelligence effort; the board said then it had no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.

Fortune and Al Jazeera, both citing other reporting, say Huang acknowledged being unfaithful to his wife and describe reported relationships, including one with a woman U.S. officials reportedly viewed as a Chinese spy. These are reports and allegations. Nvidia has not confirmed them, and whether any intelligence link exists has not been established. Fortune reports that shares fell more than 6% at first on the news. Al Jazeera reports that the market value dipped below $4 trillion, that a shareholder class action has been filed, and that Huang has reportedly agreed to testify before Congress.

Nvidia still reported record third-quarter results on 17 November, covered elsewhere on this site. The unanswered questions are who becomes permanent CEO, how long the interim period lasts, and what the board's investigation finds.
