Finyx acquires Birmingham consultancy Entec Si

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Finyx Group, a technology and transformation consultancy with offices in London, Belfast and York and more than 100 consultants, has acquired Entec Si, a Birmingham consultancy founded in 1996 by Peter Marsden, Consultancy.uk reported on 18 November 2026.

Entec Si has about 20 core staff plus associates and focuses on local government and not-for-profit clients, with work in healthcare, transport, aviation and higher education. No price or terms were given.

The deal follows Finyx's April 2026 purchase of Woodhurst, a 30-person financial services consultancy. Entec Si managing director Eman Al-Hillawi and Finyx chief executive Phil Stewart are quoted in the report.

What it means: Public-sector and not-for-profit advisory capacity is being consolidated by mid-sized technology consultancies, creating integration and local-government transformation work.
