IPO · Nov 10

Anthropic roadshow launches at $70–78; IPO later priced at $83

Open in the full site →

Anthropic announced on 10 November that it has launched the roadshow for its initial public offering. The company says it is offering 825,000,000 Class A shares, about 801 million from the company and about 24 million from selling stockholders, with underwriters holding a 30-day option on up to 123.75 million more. The expected range is $70 to $78 a share, for a Nasdaq Global Select listing under the ticker ANT. The registration statement has been filed but is not yet effective.

RTTNews reports a concurrent $26 billion private placement of Class N stock at the IPO price to partners including AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA and TSMC. Bloomberg, as relayed by StockAnalysis, says the book is several times oversubscribed and reports implied valuations of roughly $1.0 trillion on shares outstanding and $1.4 trillion fully diluted. These oversubscription and valuation figures are press-reported, not company statements.

Update, 18 November: AP reports the IPO priced at $83 a share, above the range, for 980 million shares and about $81 billion, valuing the company at about $1.5 trillion. See the separate pricing story.
