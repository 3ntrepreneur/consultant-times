Optima Partners names Rob Ward, a former Accenture and Baringa leader, as chief executive of the AI and data consultancy.

Reported

Open in the full site →

Optima Partners, an AI and data consultancy founded in 2013 by Alan Crawley, has appointed Rob Ward as chief executive to lead its next phase of growth, according to Consultancy.uk on 20 November 2026. The article says Ward has more than 25 years in consulting and strategy, including senior roles at Accenture and Baringa, with depth in financial services and energy.

The firm works with financial services, energy and life sciences clients. Consultancy.uk cites 40% year-on-year revenue growth and recent investor backing, and says Optima plans to expand its offer and presence across the UK and Europe. Ward is quoted saying organisations do not need AI for AI's sake. The report does not give a start date, say whether Crawley keeps a role, or name a predecessor.
