Detroit's Big Three line up US plant investments as 25% vehicle tariffs bite

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

A July White House release lists GM investing US$6 billion in US manufacturing including an Orion Township expansion, Ford putting US$3 billion into its BlueOval Battery Park in Marshall (1,700 jobs), and Stellantis spending US$388 million on a Van Buren Township hub plus smaller Detroit and Warren projects. The President cited the same investments in Michigan on 29 July.

The policy backdrop is a 25% tariff on vehicles and light trucks built outside the US, plus extended tariffs on foreign-built medium and heavy trucks and an offset for parts on US-built vehicles.

What it means: Auto and supplier clients face footprint decisions: where to build, what to localise and how to price tariff cost. Moves are shifting production between existing plants more than adding capacity.
