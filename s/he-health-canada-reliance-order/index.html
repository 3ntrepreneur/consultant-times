Health Canada lets reviewers rely on foreign regulators' decisions to speed drug approvals

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Health Canada announced on 15 July a package to speed access to medicines. CP24 reports a new order lets it rely on decisions or documents from certain foreign regulators, to reduce unnecessary duplication while keeping national safety, efficacy and quality requirements. Smart & Biggar reported in August that the order is now in force.

The same package opened a consultation, from 15 July to 12 September, on prioritizing certain generic submissions involving domestic manufacturing. Smart & Biggar's mid-year review also notes Canada's Drug Agency streamlined its reimbursement review process for submissions from August.

What it means: Drug makers and market-access teams may get faster Canadian launches. Regulatory strategy and submission sequencing change.

What we don't know yet

  • Approval timelines under the order are not yet published.
