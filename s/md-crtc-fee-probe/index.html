CRTC keeps pressing Bell, Rogers and Telus over new device, SIM and shipping fees

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

After the CRTC banned fees tied to activating, changing or cancelling a plan (Telecom Regulatory Policy 2026-43, effective 12 June), carriers added others. Money.ca lists a $40 device-handling fee at Bell, a $15 SIM or eSIM charge at Telus, and a $40 device-setup fee, $25 shipping fee and SIM fee at Rogers.

On 14 August the CRTC rejected a Telus request, backed by Bell and Rogers, to split the case and remove staff, keeping the review on paper. iPhone in Canada reports none of the disputed fees are struck down until the CRTC rules, so carriers may keep charging them. Compliance orders and penalties are possible.

What it means: Telecom clients must price around tighter fee rules. The case is the first test of how the ban is enforced.
