Publicis Sapient and Ping Identity team up on identity systems for AI agents

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Publicis Sapient and Ping Identity have formed a global partnership to help large organisations replace outdated identity systems and prepare for AI agents, according to Consultancy.uk on 19 November 2026. Identity systems are the technology that decides who, or what, can access applications, data and sensitive systems.

The offer combines Publicis Sapient's digital transformation, AI and software engineering work with Ping's identity management and security products. Publicis Sapient says it will use its Sapient Slingshot platform, which it describes as AI-powered software development and modernisation, to automate parts of the job: analysing existing systems, configuring new solutions and testing integrations.

A central claim is that organisations will need to give AI agents their own identities and control which applications, tools and data each can reach. Alex Laurie of Ping Identity is quoted as saying AI agents are becoming a new class of digital identity inside the enterprise. Pinak Kiran Vedalankar, group vice president for technology at Publicis Sapient, framed the aim as accelerating identity modernisation.

The companies have worked together before. Consultancy.uk cites a migration for the UK motoring organisation The AA, which moved 4.7 million users and 1.5 million active tokens to a cloud-based identity platform, which the article says happened without downtime. That figure is the companies' claim as reported, not independently checked.

Deal terms and the number of clients lined up were not disclosed. For consultants, the practical read is that agent governance is turning into a billable identity workstream, and that vendors and integrators are pairing up to sell it as a migration project.

What it means: Identity and access modernisation, agent-governance and zero-trust programmes at large enterprises; legacy IAM migrations that can be partly automated.
