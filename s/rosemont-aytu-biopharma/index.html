Rosemont Pharmaceuticals agrees to buy Aytu BioPharma at $2.70 a share

Confirmed · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Aytu BioPharma announced on 16 November 2026 that it will be acquired by Rosemont Pharmaceuticals Holdings, based in Leeds in the UK, for $2.70 per share in cash. The deal is structured as a tender offer, to be launched within ten business days, followed by a second-step merger at the same price, according to an exhibit filed with the SEC.

The company's release says the price is a 41.1% premium to the 30-day volume-weighted average price and 51.7% to the 13 November close. InsideArbitrage reports a premium of 48.35%, so the basis differs between sources; we use the company's figures. Aytu's board approved unanimously, and holders including Nantahala Capital Management and Stonepine Capital Management, plus Aytu's officers and directors, signed support agreements. Conditions include a majority tender and the exercise or termination of certain warrants, and closing is expected in late December.

Canaccord Genuity and Dorsey & Whitney advised Aytu; Bourne Partners and Ropes & Gray advised Rosemont. For advisers, the transaction shows a private specialty-pharma buyer taking a listed small cap private, which typically creates work in commercial integration, supply-chain rationalisation and financing. The release does not describe Rosemont's plans for Aytu's products, so we cannot say more.

What it means: Specialty-pharma integration, portfolio-fit and commercial-model work for acquirers of small branded-drug companies.
