Nvidia's board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after 11 days

Confirmed · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Nvidia said in an SEC filing dated 23 November 2026 that its board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang, who resigned on 12 November. Colette Kress returns to her CFO role after serving as interim CEO.

The board created a National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors and is hiring a chief compliance officer. An independent-director committee continues to investigate reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort, the filing says.

What it means: Board-governance and export-control compliance work, and supply-continuity planning for clients that depend on Nvidia chips.
