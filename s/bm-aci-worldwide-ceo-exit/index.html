ACI Worldwide CEO Thomas Warsop quits for another public company; CFO named interim

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

According to the announcement carried by MarketScreener, Warsop left as CEO effective 18 November 2026 after accepting a CEO post at a public company in a different industry, and will remain with ACI until 18 December to support the transition. CFO Robert Leibrock, who joined in July 2025 after senior finance roles at Red Hat, becomes interim CEO. The board has hired a search firm and will consider internal and external candidates.

Payments Dive reports an SEC filing says there was no disagreement behind the resignation, that ACI reaffirmed the guidance issued with its third-quarter results, and that Warsop took over in 2023 after the abrupt exit of his predecessor. It also notes net income for the first nine months fell 37% to $102.6 million.

MarketScreener's page shows the shares closing at $50.50 on 19 November, down 4.5% that day; the page does not attribute the move to the leadership change.

What it means: CEO search and succession advisory, interim-leadership governance and strategy reviews for a payments software vendor with falling profit and a pending acquisition.

What we don't know yet

  • Which company Warsop is joining was not disclosed
  • Whether Leibrock is a candidate for the permanent role is not stated
