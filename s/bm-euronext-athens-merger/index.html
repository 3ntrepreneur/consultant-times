Euronext to fold Euronext Athens into the group by statutory merger

Confirmed · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Euronext said on 26 November 2026 that Euronext Athens Holding S.A. will merge with and into Euronext N.V. Euronext Athens will cease to exist and Euronext will continue the activity through a Greek branch.

Euronext Athens shareholders would receive one new Euronext share (nominal value EUR 1.60) for every 17 Euronext Athens shares, against one for 20 in the 2025 tender offer. The release values that at EUR 9.85 a share on Euronext's close of EUR 167.50 on 25 November, which it puts 38% above the EUR 7.14 implied by the earlier offer. Shareholders who vote against and exercise withdrawal rights would receive EUR 9.29 a share in cash.

The timetable is indicative: the merger proposal is filed on 27 November 2026, extraordinary general meetings of both companies are set for 26 January 2027, and creditor opposition periods are expected to end on 2 March 2027. Completion needs shareholder approvals, Dutch and Greek publication and creditor procedures, and a Greek pre-merger certificate. KPMG Accountants N.V. and Ernst & Young (Hellas) acted on the exchange-ratio and expert reports.

The release states the exemption document is not a prospectus and has not been approved by a regulator. Whether shareholders approve, the effective date and the share delivery date are not yet known.

What it means: Post-merger integration, exchange-systems and listing-process harmonisation, and legal-entity simplification work; advisers and auditors already sit on the transaction.

What we don't know yet

  • Effective and share delivery dates
