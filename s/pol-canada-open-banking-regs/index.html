Draft open-banking regulations published in the Canada Gazette

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

The proposed Consumer-Driven Banking Regulations were published on 27 June in the Canada Gazette, Part I. Substance Law says they cover accreditation, complaint handling, technical standards, consent management and national-security review. Consultation ran to 26 August.

Red Compass Labs notes the draft implements phase one, read-only data access, and cautions that adoption assumptions look optimistic given the read-only launch. The Bank of Canada has said it will not set a date until the work is fully scoped.

What it means: Banks and fintechs should map accreditation, consent and security obligations now. A final timeline is still missing.

What we don't know yet

  • Go-live date.
  • Final accreditation criteria.
