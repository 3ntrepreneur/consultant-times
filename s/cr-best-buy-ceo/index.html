Best Buy announced the succession in April. Barry stepped down after seven years and stays as an adviser for six months. Bonfig, a 25-year veteran, oversaw merchandising, e-commerce, marketing, supply chain, Best Buy Canada and the Best Buy Ads network.

The company reported US$41.7 billion of revenue in fiscal 2026, with fourth-quarter comparable sales down 0.8%. Retail media and marketplace are the higher-margin lines the new CEO is known for building.
