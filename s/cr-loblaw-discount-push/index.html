Loblaw is betting C$2.4 billion on discount stores as value shoppers take over

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

eMarketer reports Loblaw will spend C$2.4 billion to expand and upgrade its footprint this year, and Retail Insider adds the plan covers 70 new stores, from pharmacies and clinics to discount grocery, plus renovations of nearly 200 locations.

In May the company missed first-quarter revenue estimates as lower-income shoppers cut non-essential spending, though its discount banners, including No Frills and Maxi, kept outperforming. Retail Insider's September note adds that the Competition Bureau's review of progress since its 2023 grocery study mentioned actions on property controls by Empire, Loblaw and Walmart Canada.

What it means: Value formats are structurally gaining share. Competitors, suppliers and landlords should expect more pressure on mid-tier grocery and on pricing.

What we don't know yet

  • Store-level returns on the discount rollout are not public.
