Canadian shoppers keep spending, but more selectively

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Retail Insider's Q3 consumer report says spending held up through the third quarter, but household finances pushed shoppers to allocate money very differently. A separate September piece puts 2026 consumer spending on track for about $1,485.2 billion, real growth of roughly 1.5%, and warns the average misleads.

Food costs are a big part of the story. Retail Insider's grocery report notes that store-bought food prices were up 4.3% year over year in May and had outpaced headline inflation for 16 straight months. It also says Loblaw's hard-discount comparable sales were close to 4% in the second quarter, and that SportChek posted 8.0% comparable-sales growth in Canadian Tire's latest quarter.

What it means: Retail clients need segment-level plans. A national average hides sharp differences between value shoppers and affluent households.
