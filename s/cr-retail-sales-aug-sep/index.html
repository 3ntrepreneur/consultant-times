Canadian retail sales rebound in August and an early estimate points to another gain in September

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Statistics Canada reported that retail sales rose 1.2% to $74.6 billion in August, with gains in all nine subsectors, and volumes also up 1.2% (Retail Insight Network). The gain followed a 0.7% fall in July to $73.7 billion, the first drop since December 2025, according to the Canadian Press.

Trading Economics, summarizing the agency's advance estimate, says September sales likely rose 0.7%, with gains at food and beverage, general merchandise and health and personal care stores. The full September figure is due on 20 November, and the advance number is usually revised.

What it means: Retailers and brands are seeing a choppy but growing consumer. Volatility month to month makes inventory and promotion planning harder.

What we don't know yet

  • The September number is an advance estimate and will be revised.
