Teneo hires ex-CNBC technology correspondent Steve Kovach as a New York managing director in strategy and communications (Consulting.us).

Reported

Open in the full site →

Teneo has hired Steve Kovach as a managing director in its strategy and communications practice in New York, according to Consulting.us on 25 November 2026. The report says he has more than 15 years of experience, including over seven years at CNBC as technology correspondent and technology editor for CNBC.com and nearly eight years at Business Insider.

Andrea Calise, president of Teneo's US strategy and communications practice, is quoted in the report welcoming him. Teneo describes itself as having more than 1,800 people in over 50 offices.

The hire fits a pattern of advisory firms recruiting former journalists and public-sector insiders to sell communications and stakeholder advice around technology, deals and regulation. This item relies on a single trade-press report; no Teneo release was read.
