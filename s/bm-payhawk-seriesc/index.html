Payhawk raises $280 million at a $2 billion valuation in Goldman Sachs-led round

Confirmed · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Payhawk announced the all-equity round on 18 November, doubling its $1 billion valuation from 2022, per The Next Web. Goldman Sachs Alternatives' growth equity team led, all existing investors including QED, Greenoaks, Lightspeed and Hedosophia took part, and new backers include Galdana Ventures, Cogito Capital, Inveready and Invus. Total funding passes $515 million, and the deal needs regulatory approvals, according to the company.

Payhawk says it operates in 33 countries, that 77% of customers use its AI agents daily, and that it reached positive cash flow in the third quarter. The Next Web notes revenue and ARR were not disclosed and that most revenue comes from subscriptions rather than card interchange.

The company framed the raise as building a "Finance Operating System" for the AI era; neither source gave a breakdown of how the money will be spent.

What it means: Finance-function transformation and spend-management tooling selection; AI-agent operating-model design for CFO organisations; vendor and market landscape work in fintech.

What we don't know yet

  • Use of proceeds, revenue and customer count were not disclosed
  • Regulatory approvals are still pending
