Provinces and generic makers extend their pricing agreement to 2028

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

The pan-Canadian Pharmaceutical Alliance, which negotiates for public drug plans, and the Canadian Generic Pharmaceutical Association extended their generic-drug pricing agreement for two years from 1 October 2026. It keeps the pan-Canadian tiered pricing framework and select-molecule pricing.

The generics association says generic makers supplied about 80% of prescriptions dispensed in Canada in 2025. Benefits and Pensions Monitor reports the 2023 renewal set new single-source generics at 75% of the brand reference price, with automatic drops after that.

What it means: Generic makers and pharmacies get pricing stability. Brand makers and consultants should model tiered-price erosion on new generics.
