Pfizer raises 2026 guidance as launched and acquired products grow 32%

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Pfizer reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $16.6 billion, relatively flat, with adjusted EPS of $0.87. Excluding Comirnaty and Paxlovid, revenue grew 7% operationally, and launched and acquired products grew 32% operationally. Eliquis rose 25%, driven mainly by higher US net price. A $650 million in-process R&D charge for an Innovent licensing deal cost about $0.10 a share.

Pfizer raised full-year revenue guidance by $1.5 billion at the midpoint to $62 to $64 billion and adjusted EPS to $2.95 to $3.05. On the call it said IRA pricing, 340B changes and most-favoured-nation effects will feed 2027 guidance, due around mid-December, and that it is prioritizing bolt-on acquisitions.

What it means: Big pharma is trading COVID decline for new-product growth. Cost programmes and 2027 pricing pressure drive consulting demand.

What we don't know yet
