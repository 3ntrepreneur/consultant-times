Rogers becomes full owner of MLSE and starts selling a minority stake in its sports assets

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Rogers said on 23 October that it became 100% owner of MLSE on 1 October and is pursuing the sale of a minority interest in its consolidated sports, media and entertainment assets, branded Rogers Sports, by the first half of 2027. It expects to use the proceeds to repay debt.

Media revenue rose 9% in the quarter on sports revenue and subscriber revenue from the Warner Bros. Discovery channels, partly offset by lower advertising revenue. The Blue Jays drew more than 3.3 million fans.

What it means: A template for monetizing media and sports assets inside a telco. Valuation, governance and carve-out work follow.
