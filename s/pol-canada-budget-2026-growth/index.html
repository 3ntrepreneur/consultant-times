Budget 2026 proposes a Canada Growth Incentive and consumer relief

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne tabled Budget 2026, Masters of our Destiny, on 5 November. McCarthy Tétrault says it proposes a new Canada Growth Incentive alongside GST/HST and excise measures.

Advisor.ca reports it also proposes a one-time 50% top-up to the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit, a lower income threshold for the Canada Workers Benefit and a refundable Caregiver Credit.

What it means: Corporate clients should map the incentive to capex plans once the rules are published.

What we don't know yet

  • Eligibility and size of the Growth Incentive.
  • Whether the budget passes unchanged.
