Sonoco names insider Ernest Haynes to succeed retiring CEO Howard Coker

Confirmed · 2 min

Open in the full site →

According to Sonoco's release, R. Howard Coker, CEO since 2020 and a 41-year company veteran, will stay in the role through 31 December 2026. Ernest D. Haynes III, president of Global Consumer Packaging since August 2026, succeeds him as president, CEO and director. Chairman John Haley described the change as the result of a planned succession process, per the release.

Haynes joined Sonoco in 1997 and has held roles across both its Consumer and Industrial segments, including leading metal and rigid paper packaging through the integration of major acquisitions, the company says. Paper Age carried the same announcement. Sonoco reported $7.5 billion of 2025 sales from continuing operations and has about 22,000 employees, according to the release.

The handover comes as Sonoco presents itself as a more focused business built around consumer and industrial packaging, which makes the new CEO's portfolio and capital-allocation choices the thing to watch.

What it means: Leadership-transition support, 100-day planning and portfolio review for a new CEO at a packaging group refocused on consumer and industrial segments.
