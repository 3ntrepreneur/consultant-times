Accenture's fiscal 2027 guide leans on acquisitions: about $5 billion more planned

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Earnings Whispers calculates that Accenture's guidance of 3% to 6% local-currency growth in fiscal 2027 includes 2 to 2.5 points from acquisitions, implying organic growth of roughly 1% to 3.5% versus about 3% in fiscal 2026. Management plans about $5 billion more in deals after deploying $4.9 billion across 17 deals in fiscal 2026.

Benzinga reports the first-quarter revenue guide is $18.95 billion to $19.60 billion and that Accenture plans to return at least $9.5 billion to shareholders. Analysts quoted by Benzinga are split: Susquehanna worries AI could disrupt systems integration and application development, which make up nearly half of revenue.

What it means: Even the market leader is buying capability rather than growing it organically. Boutique AI and data firms are the likely targets.
