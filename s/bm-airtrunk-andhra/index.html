Blackstone-backed AirTrunk proposes two data centre campuses in Andhra Pradesh, its India entry

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

AirTrunk has submitted a detailed project report to the Andhra Pradesh government for two campuses in Anakapalli district totalling up to 1.8 GW, according to Business Today, which puts the investment at about Rs 1.03 lakh crore (roughly $11 billion). W.Media reports the split as a 1.4 GW campus at Tootipala and a 400 MW campus at Rambilli, and says the MoU was signed with the state government.

Both outlets say the proposal is still under state review and subject to approvals and infrastructure readiness. AirTrunk told Business Today it would use low-water, air-cooled technology and draw on industrial rather than municipal water, and W.Media adds that it wants to explore renewable power from the state. Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu said the state is expanding renewable capacity and water supply to support large data centres, according to Business Today.

W.Media notes that the $11 billion figure comes from Bloomberg's reporting and that AirTrunk's own release does not state an investment amount. It also lists competing projects in the state, including Google's separately planned AI hub, which points to a crowded field for power and land in the region.

What it means: Site selection, power and water sourcing, regulatory-approval support and market-entry strategy for hyperscale operators; infrastructure financing and EPC advisory for a multi-gigawatt build-out.

What we don't know yet

  • Final investment decision and construction timetable were not disclosed
  • The $11B figure is a media estimate; AirTrunk's release gave no amount
  • Power purchase arrangements and customer anchors were not named
