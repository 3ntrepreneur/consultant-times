83% of UK firms faced financial crime attempts, but only a third hold standalone cover

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Gallagher's 2026 Corporate Crime and Risk Survey, reported by Consultancy.uk on 26 November 2026, polled 1,150 senior decision-makers in 12 countries, 150 of them in the UK. It found 83% of UK businesses had experienced attempted or actual financial crime, against 75% globally, and 57% said attempts had become more frequent.

Detection is slow: 60% said finding their latest fraud took more than 24 hours and 19% said at least a month. Some 61% saw more AI-generated content in attempts and 56% saw more sophisticated ones. The most common threat was employee theft or dishonesty (31%).

On protection, only 33% of UK businesses held standalone commercial crime insurance, about 40% relied on a combined package, and 29% said they manage their balance sheet to absorb losses. The figures come from an insurer-broker's own survey, and the article cites UK Finance on 75.6 million pounds lost to bank-transfer scams in 2025.

What it means: Fraud-controls and detection reviews, insurance-programme and risk-transfer design, and AI-enabled fraud-response work for UK mid-market and large firms.
