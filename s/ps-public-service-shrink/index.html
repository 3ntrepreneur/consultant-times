The federal public service is down to 332,000 and on track for a 10% cut

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

BNN Bloomberg reports Budget 2026 states the government is on track to reduce the civil service by 10%. There were 332,000 federal public servants as of August 2026, down from a peak of 368,000 in 2023-24.

The budget says the government will align organization operating budgets with personnel savings from the Early Retirement Incentive programme. In parallel, Digital Transformation Canada is meant to lead adoption of AI across the public service.

What it means: Fewer people and more AI: departments need operating-model, automation and service-delivery redesign. Transition and workforce planning are core work.

What we don't know yet

  • The 10% base year and timeline are not defined in the sources.
