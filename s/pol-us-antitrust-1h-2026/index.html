US merger review gets more permissive while Europe tightens digital rules

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Goodwin's first-half 2026 update describes a more permissive US merger environment alongside assertive digital regulation in Europe. It notes an abrupt departure of the head of the DOJ Antitrust Division and a reshaped FTC that is changing how it litigates merger challenges.

Arnold & Porter's second-quarter review adds that both agencies prefer divestitures to behavioural remedies, citing FTC Chairman Andrew Ferguson's stated caution about monitoring firms after a settlement.

AI runs through both reviews: it shapes how deals are structured and reviewed, and enforcers are developing theories about data, compute and talent.

What it means: Deal teams should plan for remedies and settlements rather than blocked deals in the US, and for tougher digital rules in Europe and the UK.
