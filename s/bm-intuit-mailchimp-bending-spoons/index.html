Intuit agrees to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for $3.68 billion

Confirmed · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Intuit said on 19 November 2026, alongside its first-quarter results, that it has agreed to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for approximately $3.68 billion, made up of $3.31 billion in cash and $368 million in Bending Spoons stock. Bending Spoons' own release describes an all-cash enterprise value of $3.68 billion in which Intuit reinvests $368 million into newly issued Bending Spoons equity. The two companies describe the same figures in different words, so the structure should be read from the filings. Closing is expected in early calendar year 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and customary conditions.

Intuit's release shows why the unit may have become a candidate for sale. Mailchimp revenue fell 1% to $313 million in the quarter ended 31 October 2026, while Intuit's total revenue rose 12% to $4.4 billion. Intuit said it plans to use the cash proceeds to repurchase shares in fiscal 2027 after the deal closes, and its guidance excludes the sale until then. Bending Spoons buys 100% of The Rocket Science Group, LLC and other Mailchimp assets and liabilities; its board approved the deal unanimously. Citi and J.P. Morgan advised Bending Spoons, with Freshfields as legal counsel and EY-Parthenon on financial due diligence.

Bending Spoons chief executive Luca Ferrari said the company will invest substantially in the business after closing. Neither release gives plans for pricing, products or customer data, which is what Mailchimp's customers and partners will watch.

What it means: Separation and transition-services work for the carved-out unit, integration and cost-take-out planning for the buyer, and capital-allocation advice for Intuit as it recycles cash into buybacks.

What we don't know yet

  • Exact closing date and purchase price allocation were not disclosed
