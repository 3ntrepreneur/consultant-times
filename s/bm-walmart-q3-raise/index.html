Walmart raises its full-year outlook as e-commerce and advertising keep outgrowing stores

Reported · 2 min

Open in the full site →

Walmart reported third-quarter revenue of $188.8 billion on 19 November 2026, up 5.2%, with adjusted EPS of $0.65, according to Supermarket News and StockAnalysis. Walmart U.S. e-commerce grew 23%, Sam's Club U.S. e-commerce 30%, and global advertising 25%, the first outlet reports. Global membership fee revenue rose 17%.

The company raised its fiscal 2027 outlook to net sales growth of 4.9-5.2%, adjusted operating income growth of 8-8.6% and adjusted EPS of $2.84-2.89 in constant currency, per the same reports. StockAnalysis says the sales outlook remains below analyst expectations and notes Walmart U.S. comparable sales of 2.9%. The reports flag a roughly 70 basis point headwind in health and wellness from deflation tied to drug price regulation, and pressure on Sam's Club operating income from a new bundled membership and a LIFO charge.

What it means: Omnichannel and retail-media strategy work for competitors, membership-model and pricing reviews, and supply-chain and fulfilment-cost programmes as the market leader widens its lead.
