Budget 2026 tops up the grocery benefit and adds a growth incentive for business

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne tabled the budget, titled Masters of our Destiny, on 5 November. Advisor.ca reports it proposes a one-time 50% top-up to the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit, a lower income threshold for the Canada Workers Benefit, and a refundable Canada Caregiver Credit.

McCarthy Tétrault's note for retail and consumer-products clients highlights a new Canada Growth Incentive and several GST/HST and excise measures. The firm points readers to its separate tax commentary for detail, so the exact mechanics are not set out here.

What it means: Grocers and consumer-goods makers get a modest demand support for low-income households. Retailers should read the GST/HST and excise changes with their tax advisers.

What we don't know yet

  • Size of the Growth Incentive and who qualifies were not detailed in the sources used.
  • Parliament has not passed the measures.
