Textron sells industrial segment to Kingswood Capital for $1.2 billion

Reported · 3 min

Open in the full site →

Textron agreed on 18 November 2026 to sell its Industrial segment to an affiliate of Los Angeles-based Kingswood Capital Management for $1.2 billion, according to Air Data News, PlasticsToday and Finchannel, which all report the same terms. Textron expects about $1.1 billion of net cash after taxes and transaction costs, and closing is expected in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals.

The segment includes Kautex and Textron Specialized Vehicles, whose brands include E-Z-GO, Cushman, Jacobsen and Ransomes, the outlets report. Textron will keep Textron Aviation, Bell and Textron Systems and, according to Air Data News, put the Industrial segment into discontinued operations from the fourth quarter of 2026. It expects a non-cash pre-tax impairment of $350 million to $400 million in that quarter, the report says, citing its SEC filing. The board also authorised repurchases of up to 35 million shares, per the reports, and Goldman Sachs and Latham & Watkins advised Textron, according to PlasticsToday.

FlightGlobal's earlier headline described the move as a spin-off; the trade reports describe a sale to private equity. Kingswood manages about $7.1 billion in assets, per PlasticsToday. Textron's own release and SEC filing were not readable in this run, so the figures above are as reported by the outlets.

What it means: Carve-out, separation and TSA work for Kautex and the Specialized Vehicles brands; buyer-side integration and value-creation plans; capital-allocation advice as Textron refocuses on aerospace and defence.
