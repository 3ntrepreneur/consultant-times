Corus completes its recapitalisation as lenders take ownership and a new board is named

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

The deal, first announced in November 2025, was approved by an Ontario court in March 2026 and by the CRTC afterwards. The CRTC also rejected Quebecor's attempt to buy Corus. Closing on 8 October brought in a new board.

The pressure on the business has not eased. In August Corus cut more jobs across Global News and its radio operations, and unions have criticised new job losses at Bell Media and Corus while federal support for Canadian news remains unsettled.

What it means: A restructured balance sheet does not fix the business model. Expect cost programmes, portfolio sales and distribution renegotiations; these are advisory and operations-improvement opportunities.
