US adds Megaspeed and six affiliates to the Entity List; Federal Register publication due Nov 25 (Tech Times).

The US Commerce Department added Megaspeed and six affiliates in Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand to its Entity List, according to Tech Times on 23 November 2026. Licences are needed to supply them, with a presumption of denial.

The rule is scheduled for Federal Register publication on 25 November, and shipments under earlier orders may continue for 30 days. Megaspeed denies wrongdoing, according to Tech Times. See the Policy page for the full item.
