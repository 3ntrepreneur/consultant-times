Rail merger fight enters its comment round, with critics backing a bid to end the review

Reported · 3 min read

Open in the full site →

The STB restarted the proceeding on 18 August and set the schedule: comments, protests and requests for conditions are due 18 November, and responses on 16 February 2027. A public hearing is planned before final briefs, with dates to be set. On 18 September the Board unanimously rejected motions for summary denial from BNSF, CSX and shipper groups, while one member wrote that the applicants still have a long way to go.

On 9 November, MLex reported that critics backed a CPKC request to end the review, arguing public statements by Union Pacific's CEO at a shippers' conference had corrupted the record. The Board has not ruled on that request, and nothing in the schedule has changed.

The competitive question centres on a pricing commitment Union Pacific and Norfolk Southern offered, which BNSF says covers under 1% of rail traffic.

What it means: A five-month comment window is when opponents and shippers build their evidence. Expect heavy demand for competition economics, network modelling and shipper impact analysis.

What we don't know yet

  • Whether the STB acts on the termination request.
  • Dates of the public hearing.
