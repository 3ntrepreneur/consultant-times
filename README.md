# The Consultant Times

Business news through a consultant's lens. Founded and edited by Thomson Le, Vancouver.

- Site: https://theconsultanttimes.com/
- Content lives in `data/edition.json`; the page (`index.html`) renders it.
- Editorial and operating rules: [RULES.md](RULES.md). Every automated edition follows them.
- `scripts/validate.py` checks each edition before it is published; `data/last-good.json` is the automatic fallback.

Curated and written with AI under published editorial rules.
